- **CAM:** Tight. Subject fills the frame. Motion already underway on frame one — **no push-in from a wide, no entrance.**
- **HOOK TEXT (edit layer, not baked into the render):** *"Wrong string for that kite"*

**C1b — the goal diagram (0:015-0:02)**

- **ACT:** PIP is in the same frame with his small diamond kite already up and steady, string held loose in one hand — same wind, right gauge.
- **CAM:** Hard cut. Static, medium-wide, held. This is the frame C8 returns to for the loop seam.

- **SFX:** String humming under rising tension, then a whip-*crack*; spool bearing spinning free; wind across grass; bed enters on the diagram cut.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked · PIP neutral

**Load-bearing flaw planted here:** the two string gauges are compared in frame 1. Every extra metre of line CHIEF feeds out raises the load on the thin one.
