## 2. EXACT CUE SHEET - what is playing, when

**Layer key:** `BGM` = music bed - `SFX` = effects - `VO` = narration - `AMB` = ambient (office hum + printer, runs 0:02-0:16; blinking light 0:17-0:27)

| # | Time range | Layer stack | BGM playing | AMB | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **0:00-0:02** | **BGM + SFX + VO** | `MUS_office_bed_v1` (enters frame 1, ~-17 dB) | -- (printer not yet running) | Shove + paper loading. **The paper slip into bypass tray must be audible** - it is the seed |
| 2 | **0:02-0:06** | **BGM + SFX + VO + AMB** | `MUS_office_bed_v1` (full, ~-14 dB) | `SFX_printer_whir_v1` enters at -20 dB | Counter climbing + PRINT slam + printer starts |

| 4 | **0:11-0:16** | **BGM + SFX + AMB** *(VO ends ~0:14)* | `MUS_office_bed_v1` - **brightest, densest point** (all layers) | whir at -16 dB, straining pitch | Paper mountain + overheat signs. Bed at maximum energy |

## 3. The silence beat (0:17-0:27) - the single most important audio move

A **10-second musical silence**, cut **mid-phrase** on the frame the printer dies.

**Only these sounds are permitted inside it:**

| Time | Sound | Level | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:17-0:27 | `SFX_blink_click_v1` (continuous, every 1.5 s) | **-22 dB** | The red light clicking. The machine is dead but present. An indifferent corpse |
| 0:17 | `SFX_overheat_clunk_v1` | -8 dB | The mechanical death. Exposed by the music cut |
| 0:17.5 | `SFX_power_down_v1` | -12 dB | The printer dying - descending whine |
| 0:22 | `SFX_paper_rip_v1` | **-10 dB** | First desperate yank - paper tears. Loud and harsh |
| 0:23 | `SFX_panel_clack_v1` | -14 dB | First panel opened |
| 0:23.5 | `SFX_paper_crumple_v1` | -12 dB | Pulling out crumpled wads |
| 0:24 | `SFX_panel_clack_v1` | -14 dB | Second panel opened |
| 0:24.5 | `SFX_paper_crumple_v1` | -11 dB | More crumpled wads |
| 0:25 | `SFX_panel_clack_v1` | -14 dB | Third panel opened |
| 0:25.5 | `SFX_paper_crumple_v1` | -10 dB | Even more wads (louder = more desperate) |
| 0:26 | `SFX_palm_slap_metal_v1` | **-9 dB** | The frustrated slap. The loudest sound in the silence - total defeat |

- **No VO** in this window except an optional whispered *"...it wasn't coming back."* at ~0:26.5, <=0.8 s.
- **No music, no stings.** The paper rips and mechanical sounds in silence carry the desperation.
- **The contrast principle:** harsh tearing and metallic slaps in silence vs. the warm clean print sound in C7. The *simple one-page job* defeating the *999-copy overload*.

## 5. Loudness / delivery (platform-ready)
- Integrated target **~-14 LUFS**, true-peak **<=-1 dBTP**.
- **Duck BGM ~4-6 dB under VO** for VO lines; release fully into the silence.
- VO peaks ~-12 to -10 dBFS, ~3 dB above the ducked bed.
- **The silence owns the room.** Paper rips and the metal slap escalate from -10 to -9 dB across three attempts. Leave room above the slap so the C7 clean-print sound can top it at -7 dB.
- **The protected sounds** are the **0:01 paper slip** (the seed - if it is inaudible, the fair-play contract breaks) and the **0:28 clean print** (the payoff - one smooth page defeating 999 jammed copies). Neither may be masked.
- **The loudest moments, in order:** the **0:28 clean print** (-7) -> the overheat clunk (-8) -> the frustrated slap (-9). The clean print must feel like relief after the escalating destruction.
- **Never compress the silence up.** 0:17-0:27 has no music; the paper rips and slap should feel exposed and desperate against the dead quiet, not loud. Push the blink-click to -22 dB so the rips sit forward.
- Shorts are watched **muted by default** - verify one final time that the paper tower, overheating printer, red X thermometer, and one clean page tell the whole story with audio off.
