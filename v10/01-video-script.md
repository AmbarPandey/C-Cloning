> **Video ID:** `IPPA_0010_printer_shorts_v1` - **Format:** YouTube Short, **9:16, 1080x1920**, 30 fps - **Runtime:** ~32 s
> **Idea source (computed):** **A10** - Mode A deterministic - Goal *Reach* -> Behavior *Share* -
> **NP2 Underdog Reversal** -> **SC4 Workplace/Office** -> **CM-C4 Own Tool Backfire** -> **TW4 False Victory**
> -> FM Plot-Twist - **FinalScore 8.5** - Confidence **High**.
> **Channel:** **IPPA** - *"The jerk always gets it."*
> **Cast:** CHIEF (Bully/Antagonist) - PIP (Hero/Underdog).
> **Premise:** In a tiny office, CHIEF hogs the only printer by loading an absurd tower of blank paper
> and queuing 999 copies of his "Employee of the Month" poster. He blocks PIP's single-page printout.
> But the overloaded printer overheats, jams, and resets. On reboot, the queue is cleared and PIP's
> patient single job - submitted before the jam - prints first from the bypass tray that CHIEF never
> noticed. CHIEF's 999-copy vanity project is gone.
> **Seeds:** **(A) the bypass tray** - a small pull-out tray on the printer's side, visible from C1 where
> PIP quietly places his single sheet (easy to miss because CHIEF's tower dominates the frame), and
> **(B) the "999" on the screen** - CHIEF's absurd copy count, visible from C2, which guarantees the
> overheat.
> Mute-readable; VO is an optional amplifier.

## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 - HOOK + SEED (0:00-0:02)
- **VIS:** A tiny `PAPER`-coloured office. One printer on a desk, glowing `BRAND_YELLOW`. CHIEF bulldozes past PIP (who stumbles slightly) and slams a comically tall **tower of blank paper** into the main paper tray. While CHIEF gloats at his paper stack, PIP quietly reaches over and slips **a single sheet into the small bypass tray** on the printer's side (SEED A). The bypass tray is small and easy to miss because CHIEF's paper tower dominates the frame.
- **ACT:** CHIEF shoves, loads paper aggressively. PIP slips one sheet into the side tray in a quick motion.
- **CAM:** Static wide establishing (this exact framing returns in C8).
- **VO:** *"One printer. One problem."*
- **SFX:** Office ambiance; a heavy paper-stack *thud*; a tiny paper *slip* as PIP loads the bypass tray; upbeat comedy bed enters.
- **EMO:** CHIEF aggressive - PIP patient.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium shot of CHIEF at the printer's control panel. He jabs the copy-count button repeatedly. The small screen shows the number climbing: 001... 050... 100... 500... **999**. He slams the big green PRINT button. The printer whirs to life.
- **ACT:** CHIEF holds the button, watching the number climb with widening grin. Slams PRINT. Turns and wags his finger at PIP.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in ending on the "999" counter and CHIEF's smug face side by side.
- **VO:** *"Nine hundred and ninety-nine copies."*
- **SFX:** Rapid digital *click-click-click* (counter climbing); a satisfying *SLAM* on the print button; printer whirring to life; a dismissive chuckle.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug - PIP patient.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** Close-medium on the printer's output slot. Pages fly out rapidly - all showing CHIEF's face with "EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH" in a decorative frame. CHIEF catches them with both hands, fanning them out like a card dealer, admiring his own face printed over and over.
- **ACT:** CHIEF fans pages, poses with them, holds one up next to his real face for comparison. PIP waits with one hand near his scarf.
- **CAM:** Static close-medium on the printer output; quick cuts to CHIEF posing.
- **VO:** *"His best angle. Obviously."*
- **SFX:** Rapid *shk-shk-shk* of pages printing (4-5 bursts); paper fanning; a smug "mm-hmm"; printer motor running full speed.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating - PIP content.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** Wide shot. Printed pages now pile up around CHIEF's feet - he is knee-deep in his own face. The printer's `BRAND_YELLOW` glow has shifted to show `ALERT_RED` at the edges. A small heat-shimmer appears above the machine (flat wavy lines). PIP tilts his head, noticing the red glow.
- **ACT:** CHIEF stands triumphant atop his paper mountain, arms spread. The printer shudders slightly. PIP tilts head.
- **CAM:** Static wide; slight push-in on the second half framing CHIEF on his paper mountain with the overheating printer behind him.
- **VO:** *"Still printing."*
- **SFX:** Printer motor straining (lower pitch, struggling); paper pile rustling; a small mechanical groan; bed building to peak.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant - PIP observant.

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** The printer screen flashes a **red thermometer icon** (overheating). The machine shudders violently (3-frame shake cycle). A loud **CLUNK** - total paper jam. The printer goes dark. Then a single `ALERT_RED` blinking light pulses. Pages are frozen mid-feed, crumpled and stuck in the rollers. CHIEF's face drops from triumph to horror.
- **ACT:** CHIEF reaches for the printer, hands out - his paper tower collapses around his feet. His expression crashes.
- **CAM:** Medium shot, slight low angle. The dead printer and CHIEF's fallen expression share the frame.
- **VO:** *"Uh oh."* -> then silence.
- **SFX:** Bed swells to peak then **CUTS TO SILENCE**; a loud mechanical *CLUNK*; the whirring dies (descending pitch to nothing); one blinking *click* of the red light; paper crumpling.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked (L3).

### C6 - PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** In near-silence, CHIEF yanks at jammed paper - it **rips** with a loud tear, making the jam worse. He opens every access panel on the printer (3 panels flip open). He pulls out crumpled wads of paper. He slaps the printer in desperation - the red light just keeps blinking. The **bypass tray** on the side remains untouched and unnoticed throughout. PIP raises one eyebrow.
- **ACT:** Three desperate attempts to fix the jam, each more frantic. After the slap, he slumps. PIP raises eyebrow.
- **CAM:** Static two-shot (CHIEF at printer on left, PIP standing back on right); 3-frame hold on each rip/pull/slap.
- **VO:** (silence) optional whisper: *"...it wasn't coming back."*
- **SFX:** Silence except: red-light *click* (continuous, every 1.5 s); paper **rip** (loud, harsh); panel *clack* x3; crumpled paper *rustle*; one desperate palm *slap* on metal; PIP's single blink.
- **EMO:** CHIEF frantic - PIP curious.

### C7 - TWIST / UNDERDOG REVERSAL (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** The printer suddenly **reboots** - screen flashes white, then `BRAND_YELLOW`, a startup chime. The main tray is visibly empty (all paper jammed/ruined). But the **bypass tray** whirs to life - PIP's single sheet feeds through with a clean *shk*. One perfect page slides into the output tray. The screen shows "QUEUE: 0" - CHIEF's 999-copy job has been wiped by the reset. PIP calmly picks up his page, holds it neatly, and smiles.
- **ACT:** PIP steps forward, picks up his single printed page. CHIEF's jaw drops - he looks at the screen, then at PIP's page, then at his mountain of crumpled jammed paper. PIP's face shifts from calm to subtly gleeful.
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in on the bypass tray feeding the single sheet, then settle wide; 0.5 s freeze on PIP holding his page with CHIEF surrounded by paper carnage.
- **VO:** *"One page. First in queue."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; printer reboot chime (warm, ascending); a clean *shk* of one page printing; paper sliding into output tray; a final *ding*; record-scratch as CHIEF's jaw drops.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked - PIP gleeful. <- the SHARE trigger.

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. PIP walks away with his single page, neat and tidy. CHIEF sits defeated, surrounded by a mountain of crumpled jammed paper, holding a torn shred in one glove. The printer screen shows a happy idle icon (smiley). PIP gives a small wave to camera.
- **ACT:** CHIEF slumps. PIP waves. One last crumpled page falls off the pile onto CHIEF's head.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out <=1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** A warm resolve chord; one last paper *rustle* falling; CHIEF's defeated sigh; button *ding*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP kind. **Loop seam:** C8 reuses C1's exact framing, background plate and desk position - only the paper mountain and the happy printer screen differ, so the replay re-reads the bypass tray moment.
