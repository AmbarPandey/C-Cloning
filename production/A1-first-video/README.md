# Idea A1 — First Video Production Package

This is the complete, filled production package for **Idea A1**, the item at the top of the
Mode-A queue in [Stage 4](../../docs/13-stage-4-idea-generator.md) and the designated first
video. Everything here is *compiled output* — it invents nothing beyond the locked brief.

## The idea in one line
> A smug parking-warden (**CHIEF**) gleefully boots and tickets a tiny, innocent Pal (**PIP**);
> at the peak of his overconfident victory pose, a tow truck hauls away **CHIEF's own** scooter —
> the one he illegally parked in the no-parking zone in the very first frame — using **his own boot**.
