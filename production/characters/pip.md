# Model Sheet — PIP

> **This is PIP's locked visual spec.** PIP's full character profile (purpose, narrative role,
> personality, movement/gesture/pose/expression philosophy, boundaries, evolution rules) lives in the
> [Character Bible → PIP](../design/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md#pip). This sheet is the authoritative source for
> the exact design values below.

- **Asset ID:** `CHAR_PIP_v1`
- **Role archetype:** Small, kind underdog / protagonist (the sympathetic Pal the audience roots
  for; the victim of CHIEF's power abuse who is vindicated by the twist).
- **Function in the system:** the recurring underdog for [NP1 Comeuppance](../../intelligence/06-narrative-pattern-library.md) and [NP2 Underdog](../../intelligence/06-narrative-pattern-library.md) patterns; the audience-surrogate whose relief drives the **Share** behavior.

## Silhouette & build
Tiny, soft, rounded — the visual opposite of CHIEF. ~2 heads tall, big head, big eyes, little
stub limbs. Reads as "small and harmless" in silhouette so the power imbalance is instant.

## Design spec

| Feature | Spec |
|---|---|
| Body | Small, round, gentle slouch; timid stance |
| Outfit | Simple `PAPER` tee + tiny scarf in `POP_TEAL` (visually linked to the cast, not the uniform) |
| Face | Oversized eyes, tiny mouth, soft brows; highly expressive |
| Palette | Body `PAPER` · scarf `POP_TEAL` · outline `INK` |

## Signature props (reusable asset IDs)
| Prop | ID | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny scooter | `PROP_pip_scooter_v1` | The vehicle CHIEF unjustly boots/tickets |
| Coin purse | `PROP_pip_coins_v1` | Fumbles coins at the meter in the hook (establishes innocence) |

## Expression pack
`CHAR_PIP_expr_neutral · _worried · _teary · _hopeful · _relieved · _wave`
(default resting face = **worried**; ends on **relieved/wave**).

## Personality (for pose/timing, not dialogue)
Gentle, law-abiding, easily bullied but not pathetic — quietly dignified. PIP never retaliates;
the *twist* (karma), not PIP, delivers justice. This keeps the tone advertiser-safe and likeable.

## Consistency notes
- Scarf is PIP's silhouette accent; keep it in every shot.
- PIP does **not** cause CHIEF's downfall — PIP simply endures, then reacts with relief. This is
  central to the [NP1 Comeuppance](../../intelligence/06-narrative-pattern-library.md) tone.
- Final beat: PIP peels off the boot and gives a small wave — the calm, likeable button.
