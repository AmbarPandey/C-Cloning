# Template — Thumbnail Spec

Reusable rules for the channel thumbnail (grid/browse). Fills audit finding **F6**. Filled example:
[A1 publish package](../A1-first-video/08-publish-package.md).

> Shorts autoplay in-feed, but a thumbnail still appears on the channel grid, search, and browse —
> and it drives CTR there (a top objective in [Objectives](../../docs/02-objectives.md)).

## Composition rules
- **Aspect:** design at 1080×1920 (9:16); also export a **1280×720 center-safe crop** for the grid.
- **One clear subject** (usually the antagonist mid-flex) large on one side; leave the twist implied,
  not spoiled.
- **Face-forward emotion:** use an oversized expression from the character's pack (e.g., `smug`).
- **Depth clue:** keep the seed/threat subtly visible (e.g., the `ALERT_RED` zone or an incoming prop).
- **Palette:** brand tokens only (see [style guide](../characters/cast-style-guide.md)); high contrast.

## Text rules
- **Optional, ≤3 words**, in `INK` on a `BRAND_YELLOW` pill.
- Never cover faces or the seed clue.
- Curiosity, not spoiler (e.g., `WRONG MOVE`, not `HE GETS TOWED`).

## Safety
- Advertiser-safe; no gore, no clickbait that the video doesn't pay off.

## Export checklist
- [ ] 1080×1920 still exported
- [ ] 1280×720 center-safe crop exported
- [ ] Text ≤3 words, readable at grid size
- [ ] Faces + seed clue unobstructed
- [ ] Palette tokens exact
