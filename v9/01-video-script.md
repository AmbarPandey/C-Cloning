> **Premise:** A small restaurant table. One pizza slice remains on a shared plate. CHIEF immediately starts
> building a **fortress of condiment bottles** around the plate to claim it, stacking them higher and more
> elaborately while glaring at PIP. Then he leaves the table to fetch extra toppings from the counter.
> While he is gone, PIP simply **raises his hand and politely asks the waiter**, who picks up the slice,
> puts it on a fresh plate, and serves it to PIP with a smile. CHIEF returns with his toppings to find his
> elaborate fortress guarding a completely empty plate.
> **Seeds:** **(A) the waiter** standing attentively in the background of C1 (the normal, obvious solution
> that CHIEF ignores), and **(B) the topping bar** visible at the counter behind them (where CHIEF will go,
> leaving the slice unguarded). Mute-readable; VO is an optional amplifier.

## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 - HOOK + SEED (0:00-0:02)
- **VIS:** A small `PAPER`-coloured restaurant table. A shared round plate with **one triangular pizza slice** on it (golden, with visible toppings). CHIEF's gloved hand slams a `ALERT_RED` ketchup bottle down next to the plate, possessively. Behind them in soft focus: a **waiter** (simple flat silhouette with a small `PAPER` apron) standing attentively near a serving station (SEED A). Further back: a **topping bar** with small bowls of olives, peppers, and cheese visible at the counter (SEED B).
- **ACT:** CHIEF slams the bottle down and spreads his arms wide over the plate: *this is mine*. PIP, sitting across the table, leans forward slightly looking at the slice.
- **CAM:** Static wide establishing (this exact framing returns in C8).
- **VO:** *"One slice left."*
- **SFX:** A heavy bottle *thunk* on table; upbeat trattoria-style comedy bed enters; a small "hmm" from PIP.
- **EMO:** CHIEF possessive - PIP hungry.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium two-shot across the table. CHIEF places a second bottle (mustard, `BRAND_YELLOW`), then a third (hot sauce, `ALERT_RED`), forming a wall between PIP and the pizza.
- **ACT:** He grins at PIP with each placement - a slow, possessive grin. After the third bottle, he taps the top of one smugly. PIP glances at the slice, then his eyes drift to the waiter standing behind CHIEF.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in ending on the three bottles framing the slice like prison bars.
- **VO:** *"He had a plan."*
- **SFX:** *Clunk*... *clunk*... *clunk* (three bottle placements, evenly spaced); a smug "heh"; the bed builds gently.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug - PIP thoughtful.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** The fortress grows absurdly. CHIEF adds mustard, a salt shaker, a pepper mill, a napkin dispenser, stacking them in an increasingly elaborate tower-wall arrangement. He steps back to admire his creation, hands on hips.
- **ACT:** Four more items added with increasing theatricality. After the final placement he does a satisfied nod. The waiter is still visible in the background, standing ready, notepad in hand. PIP sits patiently, hands on the table.
- **CAM:** Static wide; 3-frame hold on each new item placed. A 6-frame hold on the finished fortress.
- **VO:** *"Secure."*
- **SFX:** *Clunk-clunk-clink-thud* (four placements ascending in weight); a satisfied grunt; the bed adds a playful pizzicato layer.
- **EMO:** CHIEF obsessive - PIP patient.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** CHIEF notices the topping bar at the counter. His eyes go wide with desire. He turns to PIP and raises a warning gloved finger (don't. touch.), then turns and **walks away from the table** toward the counter, chest out, confident the fortress is sufficient.
- **ACT:** A deliberate warning finger-point at PIP, then a confident 6-step walk away. PIP watches him go, then looks at the fortress, then looks at the waiter. The slice sits unguarded (behind bottles, but unguarded).
- **CAM:** Static wide; slight push-in following CHIEF's exit, ending with the table (PIP + fortress + waiter in background) centered.
- **VO:** *"He needed toppings."*
- **SFX:** A warning "tsk-tsk"; confident footsteps receding; bed at maximum energy; one quiet plate clink.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant - PIP patient.

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** Split composition: CHIEF at the counter (background, smaller), back turned, carefully placing olives, peppers, and shredded cheese onto a small plate, taking his sweet time and arranging them artfully. In the foreground: the table with the bottle fortress, the slice inside it, and PIP sitting quietly.
- **ACT:** CHIEF is engrossed in his topping selection (slow, theatrical placements). He doesn't look back. PIP sits still - a 6-second held tableau.
- **CAM:** Static wide (the split between foreground table and background counter is the frame). Slight low angle to make the fortress loom.
- **VO:** *"He took his time."* -> then silence.
- **SFX:** Bed swells to peak then **CUTS TO SILENCE**; distant topping *plink-plink* (olives dropping); only the ambient restaurant murmur remains faintly.
- **EMO:** CHIEF self-satisfied (L3) - PIP waiting.

### C6 - PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** In near-silence at the table, PIP simply **raises his hand**. The waiter walks over (4-step, calm). The waiter picks up the pizza slice, places it on a fresh small plate, and sets it in front of PIP with a small nod. PIP takes a polite bite. The bottle fortress remains standing, undisturbed - guarding an **empty plate**.
- **ACT:** The entire sequence is calm, quiet, and polite. No drama. The waiter and PIP exchange a small smile. CHIEF is visible in the background, still at the counter, oblivious.
- **CAM:** Static medium on the table; 4-frame hold on the moment the slice leaves the shared plate, and a 4-frame hold on the empty plate inside the fortress.
- **VO:** (silence) optional whisper: *"...he just asked."*
- **SFX:** Silence except: PIP's quiet hand-raise (a tiny fabric rustle); the waiter's calm footsteps (3 soft steps); plate being set down (gentle *clink*); a small satisfied bite *crunch*; distant topping *plinks* from CHIEF at the counter. **No emphasis - the quietness is the joke.**
- **EMO:** CHIEF (away, oblivious) - PIP content.

### C7 - TWIST / IRONY REVERSAL (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** CHIEF returns triumphantly, holding his plate of artfully arranged extra toppings high like a trophy. He approaches the table. He sees the fortress - still standing, untouched, perfect. He smiles. He looks *inside* the fortress - **the shared plate is completely empty.** He looks at PIP - who is calmly chewing the last bite, a small satisfied expression. His toppings plate tilts in his shocked grip and the olives, peppers, and cheese scatter across the table.
- **ACT:** CHIEF's face runs the 3-stage snap: triumphant -> confused -> shocked. The toppings scatter is a 4-frame slide. PIP chews calmly, one hand dabbing his mouth with a napkin.
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in on CHIEF's face seeing the empty plate, then settle wide; 0.5 s freeze on CHIEF holding useless toppings beside PIP finishing the last bite.
- **VO:** *"The fortress was perfect. The slice was gone."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; confident footsteps returning; a plate *clink* (toppings set down); a beat of silence as he looks inside the fortress; the toppings **scatter** (a dozen small *clicks* and *rolls*); record-scratch; one olive rolling off the table edge with a final *plop*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked - PIP gleeful. <- the SHARE trigger.

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. Same table. The bottle fortress still standing (undisturbed and useless). The shared plate empty. PIP dabs his mouth with a napkin, polite and satisfied. CHIEF stands holding his toppings plate (now mostly empty, things having scattered), staring at the empty plate inside the fortress. The waiter is still standing in the background, smiling. PIP gives a small wave to camera.
- **ACT:** PIP dabs, then waves. CHIEF slumps. One last olive rolls slowly off the table.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out <=1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** A warm resolve chord; one rolling olive *click-click-plop*; napkin dab; button *ding*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP kind. **Loop seam:** C8 reuses C1's exact framing, background plate, table, and waiter position - only the empty shared plate, the scattered toppings, and PIP's satisfied expression differ, so the replay re-reads the waiter's presence and the topping bar.
