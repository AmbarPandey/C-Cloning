## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF + GOAL DIAGRAM (0:00-0:02)

> **Cold-open inversion.** C1 does not establish anything. It shows the ending already in motion, then
> hard-cuts to the drawn rule of the world. No character enters frame. Nothing is explained.

**C1a — the cold payoff (0:00-0:015)**
- **VIS:** Close on a lift button panel with a gloved thumb jabbing it, fast and repeatedly — and on the fourth jab the panel **sparks**, a flat `BRAND_YELLOW` flash, and every light on it dies at once. The car lurches. Cut on the lurch.
- **CAM:** Tight. Subject fills the frame. Motion already underway on frame one — **no push-in from a wide, no entrance.**
- **HOOK TEXT (edit layer, not baked into the render):** *"He mashed the close button"*

**C1b — the goal diagram (0:015-0:02)**
- **VIS:** Hard cut to the lobby: the floor indicator above the doors showing the target floor, and immediately beside the lift, a **staircase** drawn with a simple riser-and-arrow pictogram going up to the same number. Two routes, one destination, both in frame.
- **ACT:** PIP is in the same frame at the foot of the stairs, one foot already on the first riser — same destination, the slower route.
- **CAM:** Hard cut. Static, medium-wide, held. This is the frame C8 returns to for the loop seam.
- **VO:** *"Same floor. Two ways up."*
- **SFX:** Rapid button clicks, mechanical and dry; one electrical *snap*; lights dropping with a descending whine; cable groan; bed enters on the diagram cut.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked · PIP neutral

**Load-bearing flaw planted here:** the stairs are drawn beside the lift in frame 1. Abusing the close button is what strands him.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** Two-panel beat. **Panel A (lobby):** PIP faces the closed elevator doors, blinks, turns his head to look at the stairwell door, then calmly pushes it open and walks through. **Panel B (elevator interior):** CHIEF inside the elevator, pressing floor "5" button. He leans against the back wall (which has a mirror), adjusts his cap smugly, and does a little triumphant shimmy.
- **ACT:** PIP exits frame into stairwell. CHIEF gloats alone in the elevator box.
- **CAM:** Cut between lobby exterior and elevator interior (both static, same height). Push-in on CHIEF's smug face.
- **VO:** *"He didn't even look back."*
- **SFX:** Doors sealed *thunk*; stairwell door *creak*; elevator motor hum beginning; a self-satisfied chuckle from CHIEF; the comedy bed builds.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating - PIP calm.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** Interior elevator, medium shot. CHIEF stands alone, jabbing the "DOOR CLOSE" button with one index finger in a rhythmic pattern -- *click, click, click* -- even though the doors are long shut. The digital floor indicator above the doors shows "1" then ticks to "2." Around the close-door button, a faint **dark scorch mark** begins forming (the rubber seal melting slightly from friction heat).
- **ACT:** CHIEF jabs button rhythmically, looks at his reflection, adjusts his sash, keeps jabbing. Never stops pressing.
- **CAM:** Static medium; 4-frame hold on the first appearance of the scorch mark.
- **VO:** *"Still pressing."*
- **SFX:** Rhythmic plastic *click-click-click* (every 0.5 s); elevator motor hum steady; a small medal *jingle* as he adjusts his sash; bed adds a percussive layer synced to his clicking.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** The clicking **intensifies**. CHIEF now uses two fingers, then his whole palm, pressing harder and faster. The scorch mark around the button grows darker and wider. The button itself depresses deeper than its original housing -- visibly broken inward. Thin `ASPHALT`-grey smoke wisps curl from the panel edges. The floor indicator ticks "2... 3..." still crawling. CHIEF does not notice the smoke -- he is admiring his reflection, flexing his medals.
- **ACT:** Palm-slams the button repeatedly, faster and harder. Smoke rises behind his hand unnoticed.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in on the panel (100% to 106%); 3-frame hold on each escalation stage (two-finger, palm, deep-press).
- **VO:** *"Faster. Harder. Just to be sure."*
- **SFX:** Clicking becomes *slam-slam-slam*; a faint electrical *buzz* enters underneath; smoke *hiss* (very quiet); the bed reaches its densest point with a xylophone run.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant (oblivious to damage).

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** CHIEF gives one final enormous **SLAM** with his whole gloved fist on the button. A bright `BRAND_YELLOW` **SPARK** shoots from the panel. The overhead lights **flicker twice then die**. A single `ALERT_RED` emergency light activates, bathing everything in red. The digital floor indicator freezes showing a position between "3" and "4." The elevator **shudders once and stops completely.** Silence except the emergency light's hum.
- **ACT:** CHIEF's triumphant fist-pump becomes a frozen stare. His expression crashes from triumph to horror (4-frame transition). He looks at his smoking glove, then at the dead panel.
- **CAM:** Medium shot, slight low angle. The dead panel and CHIEF's fallen expression share the frame. Static hold on the frozen indicator and red light.
- **VO:** *"And then--"* (cuts off with the music)
- **SFX:** A massive electrical *CRACK*; lights *fizz* and die; a mechanical *GROAN* as the elevator stops; **music cuts to silence**; only the hum of the emergency light remains.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked (L3).

### C6 - PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** Red emergency light blinks. In near-silence, CHIEF tries to fix the situation. **Attempt 1:** He jabs every other button on the panel rapidly -- nothing responds (all dark). **Attempt 2:** He wedges his gloved fingers into the door seam and tries to pry -- the doors open a crack (showing the concrete shaft between floors) then snap shut. **Attempt 3:** He jumps with his full weight -- the elevator sways ominously on its cable with a metallic groan but stays stuck.
- **ACT:** Three escalating panic moves, each failing. Between attempts he looks up at the frozen indicator, willing it to move.
- **CAM:** Static medium; the red blink provides a rhythmic pulse to the darkness.
- **VO:** (silence) optional whisper: *"...stuck."*
- **SFX:** Dead panel *click-click* (no response); door seam *scrape* then *snap*; the jump produces a cable *creak* and a sway *groan*; the emergency light *hum-click* pulses throughout. Each sound exposed by the silence.
- **EMO:** CHIEF frantic.

### C7 - TWIST / IRONY REVERSAL (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** The elevator groans, lurches once, and slowly crawls the remaining distance to floor 5. The doors **creak open slowly** (3 seconds of agonizing slowness). Standing in the hallway is **PIP**, completely relaxed, leaning slightly against the wall beside a **hallway water cooler**. He holds a paper cup of water. He has clearly been waiting. He takes a calm sip as the doors reveal CHIEF's disheveled, sweating, panicked state. The floor hallway is bright and calm -- perfect contrast with the red emergency-lit elevator interior.
- **ACT:** Doors creep open. PIP sips water. CHIEF frozen, mouth agape. The contrast between PIP's calm and CHIEF's distress is the punchline.
- **CAM:** From inside the elevator looking out (the reverse of C1). Quick punch-in on PIP's relaxed sip, then settle wide on the contrast.
- **VO:** *"PIP took the stairs."*
- **SFX:** Music **SLAMS back** with a bright stinger; the doors *crreeeeeak* open (longest single SFX in the video); a calm water *sip*; record-scratch.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked - PIP content. **<-- the SHARE trigger.**

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition mirrors C1 (elevator doors as frame). CHIEF inside the elevator, sweating, cap askew, medals tarnished with smoke residue, the panel behind him visibly scorched. The doors are open. PIP stands in the hallway, **holding out the paper cup of water** to CHIEF -- offering it kindly, no gloating. PIP turns and gives a small wave to camera. The floor indicator finally settles on "5."
- **ACT:** CHIEF takes the cup with a trembling glove. PIP waves. One last spark pops from the elevator panel.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing (wide, static); hard cut out within 1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** A last panel *spark-pop*; warm resolve chord; button *ding*; the elevator motor finally goes silent.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP kind. **Loop seam:** C8 uses the same elevator-doors-as-frame composition as C1 -- only CHIEF's condition, the panel damage, and PIP's position differ, so the replay re-reads the stairwell door and the close button with the ending in mind.
