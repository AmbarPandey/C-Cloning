## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend — **VIS** visual/staging · **ACT** action · **CAM** camera · **VO** narration (optional layer) · **SFX** sound · **EMO** emotion

### C1 — HOOK + SEED (0:00–0:02)
- **VIS:** Wide flat contest yard. `PAPER` ground, `SKY` backdrop, simple bunting and a plain fence line in bg (no crowd). Two low `ASPHALT` platforms side by side. Between them a tall `ASPHALT` **measuring post** carrying a bright `ALERT_RED` **target band** at a modest height with a `BRAND_YELLOW` **arrow pointing straight at it** (**the seed** — the goal is: reach this line and stay standing). A small side table holds the `BRAND_YELLOW` trophy.
- **ACT:** CHIEF struts in from left, chest out. PIP stands quietly by a neat stack of blocks, sizing up the red line.
- **CAM:** Static wide establishing (this exact framing returns in C8).
- **VO:** *"The rules were simple. Reach the line."*
- **SFX:** Light comedy bed (low); a "boing" per strut step; a small wooden block clack.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug · PIP worried.

### C2 — SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium two-shot across both platforms.
- **ACT:** PIP sets his blocks down deliberately, one at a time, checking each for level. CHIEF sneers at the care, then **slaps his first block down crooked** (**the callback seed** — it sits visibly off-square) and starts piling on top of it fast, two at a time.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in on the crooked block as CHIEF's second block lands on it.
- **VO:** *"One of them listened."*
- **SFX:** PIP's careful single clacks; CHIEF's careless double-slams; a tiny wooden creak on the crooked block.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating · PIP determined.

### C3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:06–0:11)
- **VIS:** Both towers rising. PIP's is neat and plumb; CHIEF's already leans.
- **ACT:** PIP's top block settles **flush with the red band** — he steps back, content, and stops. CHIEF glances at the line, smirks, and keeps stacking straight past it.
- **CAM:** Static wide; a 4-frame hold as PIP's tower meets the line exactly.
- **VO:** *"The other one had a better idea."*
- **SFX:** A clean confirming "ting" as PIP hits the line; CHIEF's stacking accelerates.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating · PIP neutral (quietly satisfied).

### C4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:11–0:16)
- **VIS:** CHIEF's tower is now an absurd spire, swaying, far above the red band and out of the top of frame.
- **ACT:** He scrambles up his own tower and plants a little flag at the summit, arms wide. PIP watches, worried, glancing at the wobble.
- **CAM:** Slight push-in, then a tilt up the spire to the summit.
- **VO:** *"Higher. Always higher."*
- **SFX:** Rapid stacking combo; rising wooden groans; wobble creaks; a proud fanfare stab.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant · PIP worried.
- **Note:** this is the overreach — he cannot stop at "enough," and that inability is the whole mechanism.

### C5 — ANTICIPATION / THE FALSE VICTORY (0:16–0:22)
- **VIS:** At the summit CHIEF seizes the trophy and locks into an enormous victory pose, flag in the other hand, confetti drifting down. Sparkles. Everything reads **"he won."** Let the audience fully believe it.
- **ACT:** Long proud held tableau — the celebration is complete and convincing.
- **CAM:** Low hero-angle push-in that settles into the hold.
- **VO:** *"He'd won."* → then silence.
- **SFX:** Bed swells to a peak, then **CUTS TO SILENCE** (the pattern-break begins); only a faint wooden creak remains underneath.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant (L3).
- **Note:** the false victory must be sold *completely* here — a full trophy-and-confetti moment — or the reversal in C7 has nothing to invalidate.

### C6 — PATTERN BREAK (0:22–0:27)
- **VIS:** In near-silence, the camera drops to the base: the **crooked bottom block** creaks, shifts, and begins to buckle. A slow wobble ripples up the spire. High above, CHIEF holds his pose, oblivious. PIP looks up, eyes widening with hope.
- **ACT:** The creak is the joke — hold on the failing block.
- **CAM:** Hard cut to a low static insert on the crooked block, then a slow tilt up the wobbling tower.
- **VO:** (silence — let it breathe) optional whisper *"…mostly."*
- **SFX:** Silence, broken only by escalating wooden creaks, one dust-trickle, and a single suspense "tick."
- **EMO:** CHIEF oblivious · PIP hopeful.

### C7 — TWIST / FALSE VICTORY REVEALED (0:27–0:31)
- **VIS:** The spire **collapses** — blocks cascade outward in a flat tumble, dust puff. CHIEF drops with them, arms flailing, trophy launched out of his grip. The trophy arcs across frame and lands **neatly on top of PIP's little tower**, which is still standing, plumb, exactly at the red band.
- **ACT:** CHIEF's face snaps **triumphant → shocked → panicked** on the way down. PIP beams up at the trophy that just landed on his tower.
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in on the buckling base, whip-tilt down with the collapse, then settle wide on PIP's intact tower with the trophy on top; ~0.5 s freeze on that frame.
- **VO:** *"…for about ten seconds."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; huge wooden CRASH; block-clatter tail; a clean "ting" as the trophy lands on PIP's tower; record-scratch.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked · PIP gleeful. **← the SHARE trigger.**

### C8 — PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31–0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. CHIEF is **buried to his neck in his own collapsed blocks**, cap askew, his little flag drooping beside him. PIP stands beside his neat intact tower — still exactly at the red band — with the trophy sitting on top.
- **ACT:** PIP gives a small wave to camera. One last block topples off CHIEF's pile with a tiny clack.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out ≤1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; also seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** Final single block clack; warm resolve chord; button "ding."
- **EMO:** CHIEF sheepish · PIP relieved. **Loop seam:** C8 reuses C1's exact framing, background plate and horizon — only the towers, the trophy and the cast's states differ, so the replay re-reads the opening (and the crooked block) with the collapse in mind.
