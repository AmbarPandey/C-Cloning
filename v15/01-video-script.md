> **Premise:** In a quiet laundromat, CHIEF and PIP both arrive with laundry. CHIEF hogs ALL the dryers
> by stuffing his clothes into every single machine, slamming each door shut and feeding coins. PIP is
> left with no dryer. Unbothered, PIP hangs his single small shirt on a wall radiator pipe (SEED A).
> CHIEF has overstuffed each dryer far beyond capacity. The machines start rumbling, shaking, and
> overheating. One by one they pop open and violently spit CHIEF's tangled, static-charged clothes
> everywhere -- across the floor, draped over chairs, stuck to the ceiling. Meanwhile PIP's single
> shirt dries perfectly on the warm pipe. CHIEF stands in a blizzard of his own static-clinging
> laundry while PIP neatly folds his one dry shirt.
> **Seeds:** **(A) the radiator pipe** - visible in C1 with a warm shimmer, PIP hangs his shirt on it in
> C2; and **(B) the overstuffing** -- CHIEF crams clothes past the fill line in C1, audience sees the
> machines will not handle it. Mute-readable; VO is an optional amplifier.

## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF + GOAL DIAGRAM (0:00-0:02)

> **Cold-open inversion.** C1 does not establish anything. It shows the ending already in motion, then
> hard-cuts to the drawn rule of the world. No character enters frame. Nothing is explained.

**C1a — the cold payoff (0:00-0:015)**
- **VIS:** Close on a row of dryer doors **bursting open in sequence**, one-two-three-four-five, each one flinging a tangled knot of laundry out into the room — a sock arriving flat against the lens. Machines still rocking. Cut on the fifth door.
- **CAM:** Tight. Subject fills the frame. Motion already underway on frame one — **no push-in from a wide, no entrance.**
- **HOOK TEXT (edit layer, not baked into the render):** *"All five, past the line"*

**C1b — the goal diagram (0:015-0:02)**
- **VIS:** Hard cut to the laundromat: a **fill line** printed inside an open drum with a `BRAND_YELLOW` arrow at it, and the drum stuffed far past it. On the wall behind, a single warm radiator pipe with one small shirt hanging on it.
- **ACT:** PIP is in frame at the radiator pipe, smoothing his one small shirt flat on it — same laundry problem, no machine.
- **CAM:** Hard cut. Static, medium-wide, held. This is the frame C8 returns to for the loop seam.
- **VO:** *"Five dryers, and he filled every one."*
- **SFX:** Five door latches failing in a rising run; heavy tumbling thuds; static crackle; one wet slap; bed enters on the diagram cut.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked · PIP neutral

**Load-bearing flaw planted here:** the drum fill line is drawn in frame 1. Overstuffing past it is what pops the doors.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** Quick wide shot showing ALL five dryers now occupied and tumbling (round windows show rotating fabric masses). CHIEF walks along the row, patting each door like he owns them, then dusts his white gloves with satisfaction. PIP, holding his single wet shirt, looks at the row of taken dryers, then turns his head right and notices the radiator pipe. He walks over (4 steps), drapes his shirt over the pipe carefully, and smooths it flat. A tiny wisp of `PAPER`-white steam rises from where the wet fabric meets the warm pipe. PIP sits in the plastic chair, crosses his legs, and relaxes.
- **ACT:** CHIEF gloats along the row. PIP finds his alternative and sits.
- **CAM:** Medium-wide, static. The dryer row on the left, the radiator pipe and PIP on the right.
- **VO:** *"All five. Just because he could."*
- **SFX:** Dryers tumbling (muffled *whump-whump-whump*); clothes rustling; the steam *hiss*; chair *creak*; comedy bed builds with a bass clarinet layer.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating - PIP calm.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** CHIEF stands in front of the dryer row, arms crossed, chest puffed. The five dryers tumble -- but the drum sounds are labored. Dryer #1 (leftmost) starts vibrating more than the others: a visible side-to-side wobble (2-pixel oscillation). Its temperature gauge panel (a small dial above the coin slot) shows the needle creeping from green zone toward yellow zone. Through the round glass door: the clothes inside are packed so tightly they form a single solid mass rotating slowly, barely tumbling at all. CHIEF does not notice -- he is polishing his medals with a glove.
- **ACT:** CHIEF preens. Dryers strain. The wobble and gauge tell the observant viewer what is coming.
- **CAM:** Static medium, centered on CHIEF with dryer row behind him. Gauge and wobble visible but not emphasized (dramatic irony for rewatchers).
- **VO:** *"Full load. Every one."*
- **SFX:** Labored *whump...whump...whump* (slower, heavier than normal); dryer #1 *rattle* (metallic vibration); medal *jingle*; bed adds a rhythmic washing-machine-synced percussion layer.
- **EMO:** CHIEF proud.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** ALL five dryers are now shaking visibly. Temperature gauges: #1 and #2 in yellow/orange, #3-#5 creeping toward yellow. Dryer #3 (center) starts **walking forward** -- vibrating so intensely it inches away from the wall, leaving a dark gap behind it. A faint wavy `ASPHALT`-grey smell/heat line rises from the top vents of dryers #1 and #2. CHIEF notices dryer #3 out of line, frowns, walks over and pushes it back with one hand (it resists, still shaking). He returns to his arms-crossed pose. The instant he turns away, #3 inches forward again AND #4 starts walking too.
- **ACT:** The machines rebel. CHIEF pushes one back but the problem multiplies.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in (100% to 106%) across the 5 seconds, ending close on the temperature gauges climbing.
- **VO:** *"Hotter. Fuller. Faster."*
- **SFX:** *Rattle-rattle-rattle* (all five, overlapping); a *scraping* sound as dryer walks on floor; CHIEF's boots *stomp*; the bed reaches peak density with a trombone note and shaker crescendo.
- **EMO:** CHIEF oblivious (briefly annoyed, then back to smug).

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** Dryer #1's gauge needle hits the `ALERT_RED` zone. The machine shakes violently -- the whole frame vibrates. A high-pitched mechanical whine builds. CHIEF turns to look at it. The dryer door **pops open with a BANG** (the latch gives way from internal pressure). A single sock launches outward in a flat arc and sticks to CHIEF's face via static cling. **Music cuts to silence.** CHIEF stands frozen. He reaches up and slowly peels the sock off his face (it resists, static-crackling). He looks at the sock, then looks at the other four dryers -- all shaking violently, all gauges in yellow-to-red. His expression crashes (4-frame transition).
- **ACT:** First eruption. The sock on the face is the herald of what is coming.
- **CAM:** Medium shot, centered on CHIEF. The popping dryer door and launching sock are in the same frame.
- **VO:** *"Uh-oh."* (cuts off with the music)
- **SFX:** Mechanical *whine* building; door *BANG-pop*; fabric *fwip* (sock launch); static *crackle*; **music cuts to silence**; only the other dryers' ominous rattle remains.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked (L3).

### C6 - PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** Near-silence except dryer rattling and fabric sounds. Sequential eruptions:
  - **Eruption 2 (0:22-0:23.5):** Dryer #2 pops open -- a tangle of 3 shirts launches out and drapes over CHIEF's head and shoulders. He stumbles blind (can't see through the shirt on his face). Static crackle lines visible.
  - **Eruption 3 (0:23.5-0:25):** Dryer #3 pops -- two pairs of pants and underwear fly across the room. One pair sticks to the wall (static). Underwear lands on CHIEF's cap. He claws at his face, pulling the shirt off, only for the pants from #3 to wrap around his legs.
  - **Eruption 4+5 (0:25-0:27):** Dryers #4 and #5 pop simultaneously -- a BLIZZARD of assorted fabric fills the air. Socks, shirts, towels all arc outward and static-cling to every surface: the ceiling, the chairs, the floor, and CHIEF. He flails his arms trying to catch/remove items, but they stick to his gloves (static) as fast as he grabs them.
- **ACT:** Escalating chaos. Each eruption is bigger than the last.
- **CAM:** Static medium-wide. The fabric items are scattered across the entire frame by the end.
- **SFX:** Door *BANG* (x3, each bigger); fabric *fwip-fwip-fwip*; static *crackle-crackle*; clothes hitting surfaces *thwap*; CHIEF's muffled *mmph* from under shirts.
- **EMO:** CHIEF frantic.

### C7 - TWIST (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** The air clears (last few socks float down gently). CHIEF stands center-frame: completely draped in static-clinging clothes. Socks stuck to his cap. Shirts layered over his jacket. Pants wrapped around his legs. Underwear draped over his medal sash. His hair (visible under his lopsided cap) stands on end from static. His expression: frozen, wide-eyed, mouth slightly open.
  Camera pans right to reveal PIP: calmly reaching up to the radiator pipe and lifting off his single shirt. It is perfectly dry, warm, wrinkle-free (a tiny steam wisp rises as he peels it off the pipe). PIP folds it neatly: one fold, two fold, three fold (precise, calm). He places the folded shirt on his basket. He looks at CHIEF and tilts his head.
- **ACT:** The contrast delivers: CHIEF buried in his own mess, PIP's single item perfectly handled.
- **CAM:** Medium on CHIEF for the reveal, then smooth pan right to PIP at the pipe. 0.5 s hold on PIP's calm fold.
- **VO:** *"One shirt. Nice and warm."*
- **SFX:** Music **SLAMS back** at 0:27; static *crackling* (continuous on CHIEF); a warm fabric *peel* as PIP removes shirt from pipe; soft fabric *fold* sounds; resolve melody enters.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked - PIP content.

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. The laundromat wide framing: all five dryer doors hanging open, drums empty. CHIEF in the center covered in static-stuck clothes (a walking laundry pile). PIP by the radiator pipe, holding his neatly folded shirt on top of his basket. PIP extends a small flat square toward CHIEF (a dryer sheet -- anti-static). PIP turns to camera and gives a **small wave** (3-frame wave). A final sock unsticks from the ceiling and falls onto CHIEF's head.
- **ACT:** PIP offers the dryer sheet (the solution CHIEF never used), waves. Final sock is the button.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 wide framing. Hard cut out within 1 s.
- **VO:** *"Every time."*
- **SFX:** Warm resolve melody; static *crackle* (fading); sock *plop* on CHIEF's head; PIP's wave *whoosh*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP kind.
