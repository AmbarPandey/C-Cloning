## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend — **VIS** visual/staging · **ACT** action · **CAM** camera · **VO** narration (optional layer) · **SFX** sound · **EMO** emotion

### C1 — HOOK + SEED (0:00–0:02)
- **VIS:** Wide park. **Left third:** a lush shady garden — `POP_TEAL` foliage canopy, a small water fountain, cool flat shadow on the ground — with **park animals lounging exactly like people** (one reclining, one sipping, one with a tiny newspaper) as desaturated silhouette-level shapes. PIP sits on a little bench there with **BUD**, his tiny scruffy dog. **Right two-thirds:** bare, blazing `PAPER` ground under a big flat `BRAND_YELLOW` sun. **On the boundary between them:** a leaning **stack of fence panels** and an `ALERT_RED` **padlock** hanging on a post (**the seeds**). A crisp shade/sun edge is drawn on the ground.
- **ACT:** CHIEF struts in from the sunny right, chest out, and stops to stare at the shade.
- **CAM:** Static wide establishing (this exact framing returns in C8).
- **VO:** *"There was one good spot in the whole park."*
- **SFX:** Light comedy bed (low); a "boing" per strut step; cool water trickle from the shade, dry cicada buzz from the sun.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug · PIP content.

### C2 — SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium two-shot across the shade boundary.
- **ACT:** CHIEF looks at the shade, then at tiny PIP enjoying it, and sneers. He hauls the first fence panel over and **slams it into the ground** along the boundary. PIP's ears go up, worried. BUD tilts his head.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in on the sneer, ending on the first panel landing.
- **VO:** *"And someone decided it should be his."*
- **SFX:** Dismissive "pfft"; the first heavy **panel SLAM** (the signature sound); a small dust puff.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating · PIP worried.

### C3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:06–0:11)
- **VIS:** Panels going in fast along the shade line, one after another, sealing the garden off.
- **ACT:** CHIEF works with gleeful efficiency. PIP and BUD retreat further into the shade; two lounging animals lift their heads to watch.
- **CAM:** Static wide; a 3-frame impact hold on every second panel.
- **VO:** *"So he started building."*
- **SFX:** Rhythmic panel SLAMS, rising in pitch; dust puffs; a tiny worried whine from BUD.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating · PIP worried.

### C4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:11–0:16)
- **VIS:** The wall doubles in height — panels stacked on panels, absurdly overbuilt for a garden fence.
- **ACT:** He caps it off by hanging the `ALERT_RED` **padlock** on the final gate, then dusts his gloves off with two crisp claps.
- **CAM:** Slight push-in; a 4-frame hold as the padlock is hung.
- **VO:** *"Panel by panel."*
- **SFX:** Faster SLAM combo; the padlock's metallic *clink* as it's hung; a proud fanfare stab; two glove claps.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant · PIP worried.
- **Note:** the overbuild is the comeuppance engine — the more thoroughly he seals the shade, the more completely he seals himself out.

### C5 — ANTICIPATION (0:16–0:22)
- **VIS:** CHIEF plants his fists on his hips and steps back to admire the finished wall, chest out, chin high, medals gleaming. Sparkles of self-satisfaction. The wall looks genuinely impressive.
- **ACT:** Long proud held tableau. He has, as far as he is concerned, won.
- **CAM:** Low hero-angle push-in that settles into the hold — **kept deliberately tight on CHIEF so the audience cannot yet see the whole geometry.**
- **VO:** *"He thought of everything."*
- **SFX:** Bed swells to a peak, then **CUTS TO SILENCE** (the pattern-break begins); only the cicada buzz remains, and it is getting louder.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant (L3).
- **Note:** the tight framing here is the misdirection (CM-A2 in support). The twist is already true on screen — we simply haven't shown it yet.

### C6 — PATTERN BREAK (0:22–0:27)
- **VIS:** In near-silence the camera **pulls back**, and the geometry lands: the fence rings the **shade**, and CHIEF is standing **outside it**, alone on the bare, blazing side. Every animal, PIP and BUD are inside the cool green. The shade/sun edge on the ground runs right along his boots.
- **ACT:** His grin holds for a beat, then falters. He looks left, looks right, and one sweat bead pops. PIP looks up with dawning hope.
- **CAM:** Slow **PULLOUT** from the tight C5 framing to a full wide — the reveal is the camera move, not a cut.
- **VO:** (silence — let it breathe) optional whisper *"…oh."*
- **SFX:** Silence except a rising cicada buzz, one hollow gust, and a single suspense "tick." The cool water trickle is now clearly on the *other* side of the fence.
- **EMO:** CHIEF dawning doubt · PIP hopeful.

### C7 — TWIST / IRONY REVERSAL (0:27–0:31)
- **VIS:** **BUD** trots to the gate and nudges it swinging shut with his nose. **PIP** reaches up on tiptoes and **clicks the `ALERT_RED` padlock closed — from the inside.**
- **ACT:** CHIEF's face snaps **triumphant → shocked → panicked**. He grabs the fence and rattles it. Above him the `BRAND_YELLOW` sun blazes; heat lines rise off his cap. Inside, PIP beams and the animals return to lounging, unbothered.
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in on the padlock click, then settle wide; ~0.5 s freeze on CHIEF gripping the fence with the cool garden behind PIP.
- **VO:** *"He'd built it from the outside."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; a small, quiet, decisive **padlock CLICK** (deliberately tiny after all the slams); fence rattle; record-scratch.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked · PIP gleeful. **← the SHARE trigger.**

### C8 — PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31–0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. CHIEF is wilting in the blazing sun outside his own magnificent wall, cap drooping over his eyes, gloves still on the fence. Inside, PIP sits back on the bench with BUD in the cool shade, the animals lounging around them, the fountain trickling.
- **ACT:** PIP gives a small wave to camera. BUD's tail gives one wag.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out ≤1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; also seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** A last dry cicada buzz; a cool water trickle; warm resolve chord; button "ding."
- **EMO:** CHIEF sheepish · PIP relieved. **Loop seam:** C8 reuses C1's exact framing, background plate and horizon — only the fence, the padlock state and the cast positions differ, so the replay re-reads the opening (and which side of the line he was always on) with the irony in mind.
