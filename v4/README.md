**Story in one line:** one perfect shady garden in a blazing park where the animals lounge like people.
CHIEF decides the shade should be his alone and walls it off, taller and taller, finishing with a
padlock — then steps back to admire a fence he built **from the outside.** PIP quietly clicks it shut.

**The seeds:** the **fence-panel stack + hanging padlock** (frame 1), and the **shade/sun edge drawn on
the ground** that shows which side he is standing on the entire time.

**Why v4 is structurally new for the series:**
- **First fresh scenario** — SC10 Animals-as-People (v1 was SC1, v2 and v3 were both SC3), which resolves the freshness flag I raised on v3.
- **First `PULLOUT` reveal.** v1 punched in, v2 whip-followed, v3 tilted up. v4's twist is revealed by *widening the frame on a man who hasn't moved* — the C5→C6 transition is deliberately **not a cut**.
- **The twist is true from frame 1.** Nothing is hidden by plot, only by framing, which makes this the strongest replay-bait episode so far: the honest answer to "when could you tell?" is *the first frame*.
- **First inversion to a different sound.** v1–v3 inverted their signature sound into a weakened version of itself; v4 answers twelve heavy panel slams with one tiny padlock click.
