## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend — **VIS** visual/staging · **ACT** action · **CAM** camera · **VO** narration (optional layer) · **SFX** sound · **EMO** emotion

### C1 — HOOK + SEED (0:00–0:02)
- **VIS:** Wide parking lot. `ASPHALT` ground, `SKY` backdrop, parking meter left. Lower-right foreground: bold `ALERT_RED` **NO PARKING** zone with **CHIEF's scooter parked squarely inside it** (the seed — framed clearly but not emphasized).
- **ACT:** CHIEF struts in from left, chest out. PIP fumbles coins at the meter beside his tiny scooter.
- **CAM:** Static wide establishing (this exact framing returns in C8).
- **VO:** *"This is a story about a man who loved rules… a little too much."*
- **SFX:** Jaunty comedy bed (low), a "boing" per strut step.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug · PIP worried.

### C2 — SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** CHIEF spots PIP's front tire a hair over the white line. Medium two-shot.
- **ACT:** He grins, whips out the oversized ticket pad and giant rubber stamp.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in on the grin.
- **VO:** *"And today, he found his villain."*
- **SFX:** Comedic "aha" sting; paper riffle.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating · PIP worried.

### C3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:06–0:11)
- **VIS:** CHIEF stamps a ticket onto PIP's tiny scooter, then clamps a `BRAND_YELLOW` wheel boot on it with a flourish.
- **ACT:** PIP's eyes well up; CHIEF blows on his stamp like a gunslinger.
- **CAM:** Static; 4-frame impact hold on the boot clamp.
- **VO:** *"One tire over the line. That's all it took."*
- **SFX:** Loud STAMP thunk; boot CLAMP clank; tiny sad "aww" bell.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug · PIP teary.

### C4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:11–0:16)
- **VIS:** A comical mountain of tickets piled on PIP's scooter.
- **ACT:** CHIEF polishes his medals, admiring himself.
- **CAM:** Slight push-in.
- **VO:** *"He wasn't just doing his job. He was enjoying it."*
- **SFX:** Rapid stamp combo (rat-a-tat); tiny triumphant fanfare.
- **EMO:** CHIEF peak-gloat · PIP defeated.

### C5 — ANTICIPATION (0:16–0:22)
- **VIS:** CHIEF hops onto a small podium, plants a tiny flag, strikes a victory pose — stamp raised to the sky, sparkle accents.
- **ACT:** Long proud freeze on the pose.
- **CAM:** Low hero-angle rise; ~1.5 s hold.
- **VO:** *"He thought he'd won."* → then silence.
- **SFX:** Music swells, then **CUTS TO SILENCE** (the pattern-break begins).
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant.

### C6 — PATTERN BREAK (0:22–0:27)
- **VIS:** Behind the oblivious CHIEF, a tow truck rolls in, hook swinging — past PIP's scooter — toward the `ALERT_RED` no-parking corner.
- **ACT:** CHIEF frozen in pose. PIP notices, eyes widening with hope.
- **CAM:** Static wide, deep focus; suspense hold as the hook aligns.
- **VO:** (silence — let it breathe) optional whisper *"…he didn't."*
- **SFX:** Silence, broken only by a low truck rumble + a single suspense "tick."
- **EMO:** CHIEF oblivious · PIP hopeful.

### C7 — TWIST / INSTANT KARMA (0:27–0:31)
- **VIS:** The hook clamps **CHIEF's own scooter** in the red zone; the truck's arm grabs **CHIEF's own giant stamp** and slams a huge `ALERT_RED` **"TOWED"** mark on it.
- **ACT:** CHIEF's face snaps **smug → shocked → panicked**; he's yanked off the podium, arms flailing. PIP beams.
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in, then settle; ~0.5 s freeze on the "TOWED" frame.
- **VO:** *"Turns out… rules apply to everyone."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; huge comedic PUNCH on the stamp; record-scratch.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked · PIP gleeful. **← the SHARE trigger.**

### C8 — PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31–0:32)
- **VIS:** CHIEF hauled off hanging from his towed scooter, medals jangling. PIP calmly peels the popped-off boot from his scooter and gives a small wave to camera. Camera settles into the **exact C1 composition**.
- **ACT:** PIP wave; CHIEF shrinking into the distance.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out ≤1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (button; also seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** Warm resolve chord; tiny "pop"; button "ding."
- **EMO:** PIP relieved. **Loop seam:** last frame == first frame.
