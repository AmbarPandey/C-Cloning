> a single delicate page drifting down in near-silence tells you everything about what just happened.
> It is the audio equivalent of a feather landing after an explosion.

## 5. Mix notes for the editor

1. **The library setting demands a quieter overall mix** than previous episodes. Master ceiling at -6 dB (vs. -3 dB for laundromat/beach). This makes the CRACK and collapse feel even louder by contrast.
2. **Bonk rhythm in Wave 1** should feel like a xylophone phrase -- four descending bonks at decreasing intervals (musical comedy timing). Pitch-shift each bonk slightly to create a "scale."
3. **The page flutter** at 0:26.8 needs reverb (small room, ~0.8 s tail) to place it in the library space. It should sound "far away" even though it is close -- this creates the post-impact deafness effect.
4. **PIP's page turn** at 0:30 should be the cleanest, crispest sound in the entire video. It is a ASMR-quality close-mic paper sound. Use the original recording unprocessed at -10 dB. The intimacy of this sound is the audio punchline.
5. **Library ambience** is continuous and never cuts. It bridges across the music cut at 0:16 and the stinger at 0:27 -- the ambient hum is the thread that holds the silence together.
