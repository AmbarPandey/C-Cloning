# v16 - Video Script ("The Book Tower") - **SHORTS**

> **Video ID:** `IPPA_0016_library_shorts_v1` - **Format:** YouTube Short, **9:16, 1080x1920**, 30 fps - **Runtime:** ~32 s
> **Idea source (computed):** **A16** - Mode A deterministic - Goal *Reach* -> Behavior *Share* -
> **NP3 Overreach Collapse** -> **SC16 Library** -> **CM-C4 Own Tool Backfire** -> **TW3 Instant Karma**
> -> FM Plot-Twist - **FinalScore 8.5** - Confidence **High**.
> **Channel:** **IPPA** - *"The jerk always gets it."*
> **Cast:** CHIEF (Bully/Antagonist) - PIP (Hero/Underdog).
> **Premise:** In a quiet library, CHIEF commandeers the only study table, stacking books into a
> massive fortress-wall to block PIP from sitting. He piles higher and higher -- encyclopedias,
> dictionaries, atlases -- until his tower is an absurd monument to pettiness. PIP simply picks up
> ONE slim picture book and sits on a floor cushion nearby (SEED A). CHIEF keeps adding volumes, but
> the table groans under the weight. When he places the FINAL book on top (a tiny paperback), the
> table collapses, launching ALL books into the air. They rain down on CHIEF, burying him in an
> avalanche of his own literature. Meanwhile PIP sits peacefully on his floor cushion, reading his
> picture book with the quiet-zone "SHHH" sign visible above (SEED B -- the irony is CHIEF caused
> all the noise in a silent zone).
> **Seeds:** **(A) the floor cushion** - visible in C1 beside a low shelf, PIP uses it in C2; and
> **(B) the SHHH sign** - visible on the wall in C1, the irony payoff is that CHIEF's collapse is the
> loudest event the library has ever seen. Mute-readable; VO is an optional amplifier.

## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF + GOAL DIAGRAM (0:00-0:02)

> **Cold-open inversion.** C1 does not establish anything. It shows the ending already in motion, then
> hard-cuts to the drawn rule of the world. No character enters frame. Nothing is explained.

**C1a — the cold payoff (0:00-0:015)**
- **VIS:** Close on a table leg **splitting** — the joint opening, the whole tabletop dropping a hand's width, and the base of a huge book stack shearing sideways above it. One thin paperback slides off the top and hangs in the air. Cut before the stack lands.
- **CAM:** Tight. Subject fills the frame. Motion already underway on frame one — **no push-in from a wide, no entrance.**
- **HOOK TEXT (edit layer, not baked into the render):** *"One paperback too many"*

**C1b — the goal diagram (0:015-0:02)**
- **VIS:** Hard cut to the reading room: a small **load-limit pictogram** stamped on the table leg — a stacked-books shape with a line across it and a `BRAND_YELLOW` arrow at the line — with the actual stack already well past it. A floor cushion sits free beside the shelves.
- **ACT:** PIP is in the same frame on the floor cushion with one slim picture book open on his knees — same library, one book.
- **CAM:** Hard cut. Static, medium-wide, held. This is the frame C8 returns to for the loop seam.
- **VO:** *"The table had a limit. He found it."*
- **SFX:** Wood fibres tearing, low and slow; a deep joint *crack*; pages riffling; library hush underneath; bed enters on the diagram cut.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked · PIP neutral

**Load-bearing flaw planted here:** the load-limit mark is drawn on the leg in frame 1. Each added volume walks the stack closer to it.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** CHIEF rapidly pulls books from the nearest shelf (2-frame grabs) and stacks them into a WALL across the table edge facing PIP -- volumes standing upright side by side, 8 books wide, 2 rows high. The wall completely blocks access to the table from PIP's side. CHIEF steps back, dusts his gloves, crosses arms with a satisfied nod. PIP observes the wall, looks right, notices the floor cushion. He shrugs (3-frame), walks to the cushion (4 steps), sits cross-legged on it, and opens his picture book flat on his lap. The reading lamp on the low shelf beside him glows `BRAND_YELLOW` warmly, illuminating his page.
- **ACT:** CHIEF builds barrier. PIP finds alternative comfort.
- **CAM:** Medium-wide, static. Left: table with CHIEF building. Right: PIP migrating to cushion.
- **VO:** *"Every. Single. Book."*
- **SFX:** Rapid book thuds (rhythmic: thud-thud-thud-thud); shelf creaking; glove dusting *pff*; cushion *poof* as PIP sits; page turn (soft); comedy bed adds warm bass clarinet.
- **EMO:** CHIEF territorial - PIP content.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** CHIEF decides the wall is not enough. He starts building VERTICALLY. He grabs a thick green encyclopedia and places it flat on the table center. Then a red dictionary on top. Then a blue atlas. The stack grows: 6 books high, each volume visibly heavy (thick spines, gold lettering). The table creaks faintly (CHIEF does not notice). He adds two thick books sideways as angled "buttresses" against the main tower. He steps back, one hand on hip, admiring his literary monument. His medals glint in the reading lamp light.
- **ACT:** CHIEF builds upward. The tower takes shape. Table begins to strain.
- **CAM:** Static medium, centered on table and CHIEF. Tower growth is the clear visual focus.
- **VO:** *"Higher. Always higher."*
- **SFX:** Book placement thuds (deeper as stack grows); wood creaking (faint, 2 instances); medal jingle; bed adds staccato strings matching the stacking rhythm.
- **EMO:** CHIEF proud.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** The tower is now 10 books high, absurdly tall (nearly as tall as CHIEF). It wobbles slightly with each new addition (2-pixel sway, 4-frame cycle). The table legs are visibly bowing outward -- the front-left leg shows a hairline gap at the joint. CHIEF stretches on his tiptoes to place a thick atlas on the very top (his cap almost touches the ceiling). The tower sways more pronounced (4-pixel oscillation). He steadies it with one finger (it stops). He grins and immediately reaches for the shelf to grab ANOTHER book. The table emits a low wooden groan. CHIEF pauses for 1 beat, shrugs, and continues reaching.
- **ACT:** The tower reaches critical mass. Table protests. CHIEF ignores all warnings.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in (100% to 108%) ending on the bowing table leg and the wobbling tower top in the same frame.
- **VO:** *"One more. Just one more."*
- **SFX:** Book placement (strained, slow); wood bowing (low groan); wobble sound (high *eeee*); bed hits maximum density -- staccato strings + xylophone + rim clicks + ascending chromatic run.
- **EMO:** CHIEF obsessive (a hint of doubt, immediately dismissed).

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** CHIEF holds up a tiny paperback (the smallest book possible -- thin, light, harmless-looking). He reaches up ceremonially to place it on the absolute peak of the tower. His finger releases it. The tiny book settles. For 4 frames: nothing. Then the table emits a loud **CRACK** (the front-left leg snaps at the joint). **Music cuts to silence.** The table surface tilts 5 degrees. The tower leans. CHIEF's expression transforms (4-frame: smug to horrified). He lunges forward, both hands out to steady the tower -- but his touch sends vibrations through the stack. The top 2 books begin sliding. He pulls his hands back. The lean increases.
- **ACT:** The tiny book is the final straw. CHIEF's attempt to save it accelerates the destruction.
- **CAM:** Medium shot, static. CHIEF and tower centered. The crack and tilt are visible.
- **VO:** *"...oh no."* (trails off with music)
- **SFX:** Tiny book *pat*; 4-frame silence; CRACK (wood snapping, loud); **music cuts**; books sliding (paper friction); CHIEF's gasp; only the faint library air hum and sliding paper sounds remain.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked (L3).

### C6 - PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** The collapse in three waves: (1) **0:22-0:23.5** - The top 4 books slide off the tilting tower and fall on CHIEF's head one by one (each bonks his cap lower onto his face). (2) **0:23.5-0:25** - The middle section (6 books) cascades as the tilt exceeds 20 degrees -- they tumble onto his shoulders, chest, and arms. He staggers backward, arms flailing. (3) **0:25-0:27** - The table fully collapses (all four legs splay outward), and the base wall of books (8+ volumes) slides forward like a literary avalanche, hitting CHIEF at knee level. He topples backward into the pile. The mound settles: a heap of books with only CHIEF's cap visible on top (askew) and one white-gloved hand poking out.
- **ACT:** Three-wave collapse buries CHIEF under his own creation.
- **CAM:** Static medium-wide (pulled back to show full collapse path).
- **VO:** (none - silence carries the comedy)
- **SFX:** Bonk-bonk-bonk-bonk (rhythmic book hits on head); cascade of thuds (increasing density); table legs CRACK-CRACK-CRACK-CRACK; final heavy THUMP as the mass settles; a single page floats down with a soft *fft*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF buried - PIP not shown.

### C7 - TWIST (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** Wide reveal. Left side: the demolished table, splayed legs, and a large mound of books. CHIEF's cap sits on top of the mound (cock-eyed). One white-gloved hand sticks out from the side, fingers twitching weakly. A single page flutters down and lands on the cap. Camera pans right to PIP: sitting on his `POP_TEAL` floor cushion, legs crossed, picture book open, reading lamp glowing. He turns a page calmly (the most peaceful action possible). Above him on the wall: the "SHHH" sign, perfectly intact and relevant -- CHIEF just made the loudest noise the library has ever heard. PIP looks up from his book toward camera and tilts his head with a gentle smile.
- **ACT:** The contrast reveal. Chaos (CHIEF) vs. peace (PIP). The SHHH sign is the visual punchline.
- **CAM:** Medium on book-mound (beat 1), smooth pan right (12-frame) to PIP on cushion (beat 2). Hold on PIP's page-turn and head-tilt.
- **VO:** *"Every time."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back at 0:27 (warm resolve: glockenspiel + soft pizzicato); a muffled groan from under the books; PIP's page turn (crisp, clean); the ambient library hum returns to full.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP peaceful.

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1 exactly. The library wide shot: collapsed table with splayed legs, book mound with cap on top, one gloved hand out. PIP stands beside his cushion (book neatly closed, placed on the low shelf). He holds up the round "SHHH" sign (removed from the wall) in one hand and points at it with his other hand, smiling at CHIEF's mound. PIP turns to camera and gives his signature small wave (3-frame). A final book slides off the top of CHIEF's mound and thuds on the floor.
- **ACT:** Payoff -- the SHHH sign weaponized as a punchline prop. Wave. Loop.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 wide framing. Hard cut. Loop seam: shelves, table position, cushion all match C1.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (whispered, matching the library setting)
- **SFX:** Warm resolve tail continues; book slide + soft thud; PIP's wave has no sound (library silence respected).
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP kind.
