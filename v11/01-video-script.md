## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF + GOAL DIAGRAM (0:00-0:02)

> **Cold-open inversion.** C1 does not establish anything. It shows the ending already in motion, then
> hard-cuts to the drawn rule of the world. No character enters frame. Nothing is explained.

**C1a — the cold payoff (0:00-0:015)**
- **VIS:** Close on a self-checkout barrier **slamming down** across the belt, `ALERT_RED` bar dropping into place, and a counter above it flipping from `10` to **`11`** and locking. The alert light strobes. A gloved hand is still mid-reach with the twelfth item. Cut on the lock.
- **CAM:** Tight. Subject fills the frame. Motion already underway on frame one — **no push-in from a wide, no entrance.**
- **HOOK TEXT (edit layer, not baked into the render):** *"Item eleven ends it"*

**C1b — the goal diagram (0:015-0:02)**
- **VIS:** Hard cut to the lane sign: a pictogram of **ten item shapes and a cross through an eleventh** — no words. Beside it, the next register with its own light showing a clean `POP_TEAL` open state, unattended.
- **ACT:** PIP is in frame at the same lane entrance holding one small item, already turning toward the open register — same queue, one item.
- **CAM:** Hard cut. Static, medium-wide, held. This is the frame C8 returns to for the loop seam.
- **VO:** *"Ten items or fewer. He brought thirty."*
- **SFX:** Barrier *clack* and servo whir; alert tone doubling; a cart wheel squeal cut short; bed enters on the diagram cut.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked · PIP neutral

**Load-bearing flaw planted here:** the open register is lit from frame 1 and the limit is drawn on the sign. Overloading the cart is what locks the machine.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium shot of CHIEF at the self-checkout scanner. He scans items in rapid succession. The screen shows "ITEMS: 1"... "ITEMS: 2"... "ITEMS: 3". Each scan beeps. He barely looks at what he is scanning, eyes on PIP instead.
- **ACT:** CHIEF scans cockily, glances back at PIP with a smirk and a shoulder shrug: *first come, first served*. PIP looks at the right register's green light.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in ending on the counter "ITEMS: 3" and CHIEF's smug face.
- **VO:** *"One. Two. Three."*
- **SFX:** Three scanner *beeps* (cheerful); item-onto-belt *thunk* x3; a dismissive "tch"; background store ambiance.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug - PIP patient.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** Close-medium on the scanner and screen. CHIEF's hands move faster - items fly across. The counter jumps: 4... 5... 6... 7. Each beep is slightly louder and the screen numbers get slightly larger. The "10 ITEMS OR FEWER" sign is visible in the top of the frame, glowing softly.
- **ACT:** CHIEF scans in a blur. He does not look at the counter. One item nearly falls and he catches it mid-scan without pausing. PIP waits, rotating his milk carton idly.
- **CAM:** Static close-medium on the counter; 3-frame hold on each number change.
- **VO:** *"Four. Five. Six. Seven."*
- **SFX:** Faster *beep-beep-beep-beep* (ascending pitch); items sliding across scanner surface; the sign overhead gives a subtle electric hum; PIP's idle milk-carton rotation.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating - PIP content.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** The counter reads 8... 9... **10**. At "10" the screen flashes `BRAND_YELLOW` for 3 frames (a warning pulse). The overhead sign pulses once in sync. CHIEF does not notice either warning - he is already reaching for the next item from his cart with a wide grin.
- **ACT:** CHIEF grabs item 11 from his cart and holds it above the scanner, about to scan. PIP tilts his head, watching the counter hit 10.
- **CAM:** Static wide; slight push-in on the second half framing CHIEF reaching for item 11 with the "10" on the screen behind him.
- **VO:** *"Eight. Nine. Ten."*
- **SFX:** Three more beeps (8, 9, 10); the 10th beep has a *different tone* (slightly flat, a warning); the sign overhead pulses with a low *buzz*; bed building to peak.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant - PIP observant.

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** CHIEF scans item **11**. The screen immediately flashes **ALERT_RED** with large text: "OVER LIMIT". A physical **red barrier arm** drops from above (like a parking gate), blocking the exit lane. An alarm beacon spins on top of the register. CHIEF freezes mid-scan, item in hand, grin collapsing.
- **ACT:** CHIEF looks at the screen, then up at the barrier, then back at the screen. His body language deflates completely.
- **CAM:** Medium shot, slight low angle. The red barrier arm and the "OVER LIMIT" screen dominate.
- **VO:** *"Eleven."* -> then silence.
- **SFX:** Bed swells to peak then **CUTS TO SILENCE**; one loud wrong-answer *BUZZ*; the barrier arm dropping with a mechanical *CLUNK*; alarm beacon starting (one rotation click); items settling in the cart.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked (L3).

### C6 - PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** In near-silence, CHIEF tries to fix it. Attempt 1: re-scans item 11 (screen flashes "OVER LIMIT" again with a buzz). Attempt 2: jabs the touchscreen buttons frantically (no response, screen stays red). Attempt 3: grabs the red barrier arm with both hands and tries to lift it (it does not budge - it clicks but holds firm). The right register's green "OPEN" light blinks in the background, patient and visible but unnoticed by CHIEF. PIP raises one eyebrow.
- **ACT:** Three failed attempts, each more desperate. After the barrier-pull fails, CHIEF slumps against it.
- **CAM:** Static two-shot (CHIEF trapped at express lane on left, PIP and open register on right); 3-frame hold on each "OVER LIMIT" flash.
- **VO:** (silence) optional whisper: *"...he was stuck."*
- **SFX:** Silence except: alarm beacon *click* (continuous, slow rotation); re-scan *buzz* (harsh); screen button *tap-tap-tap* (dead, no response sounds); barrier rattle (metallic, stuck); PIP's single blink.
- **EMO:** CHIEF frantic - PIP curious.

### C7 - TWIST / OVERREACH COLLAPSE (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** PIP calmly walks over to the **right register** (the one with the green "OPEN" light). He places his single milk carton on the scanner. One clean **beep**. The screen shows "ITEMS: 1 - TOTAL: $2.49 - THANK YOU" in friendly green. The exit gate on the right side opens smoothly. PIP picks up his small bag, steps through. CHIEF's jaw drops - he is still trapped behind the red barrier, his overflowing cart mocking him.
- **ACT:** PIP walks (4 steps), places one item, one beep, picks up bag, walks through. CHIEF's jaw drops. The express lane screen still shows "OVER LIMIT."
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in on PIP's single item scanning (the clean beep), then settle wide; 0.5 s freeze on PIP walking through the open gate with CHIEF still trapped behind the red barrier.
- **VO:** *"One item. One beep."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; PIP's footsteps (light, 4 steps); one clean satisfying scanner *beep*; a cheerful *ding* from the register; exit gate slides open (smooth mechanical sound); record-scratch as CHIEF's jaw drops; a small item settling in the bag.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked - PIP gleeful. <- the SHARE trigger.

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. PIP walks away with his small bag. CHIEF is still trapped behind the red barrier arm, his overflowing cart next to him. The express lane screen still flashes "OVER LIMIT." The "10 ITEMS OR FEWER" sign is clearly visible above CHIEF. PIP gives a small wave to camera.
- **ACT:** CHIEF slumps against the barrier. PIP waves. One item falls off CHIEF's overloaded cart.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out <=1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** A warm resolve chord; one item *thunk* falling from cart; CHIEF's defeated groan; button *ding*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP kind. **Loop seam:** C8 reuses C1's exact framing, background plate and register positions - only the red barrier across CHIEF's lane and PIP's absence from the scene differ, so the replay re-reads the sign and the green "OPEN" light.
