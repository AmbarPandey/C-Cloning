## The name

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Channel name** | **IPPA** |
| **Written form** | All caps, always. `IPPA` — never *Ippa*, *iPPA*, or *I.P.P.A.* |
| **Pronunciation** | **IP-puh** — `/ˈɪpə/`. Short *i* as in "sit," soft unstressed ending. |
| **Syllables** | 2 |
| **Tagline (primary)** | **The jerk always gets it.** |
| **Tagline (alt, neutral contexts)** | Karma in 30 seconds. |

### Why the name works

1. **It's PIP rearranged.** `IPPA` is the letters of [PIP](../production/characters/pip.md) — the
   series lead and channel face — with an `A` added. The channel is named after its star. That gives
   you a real origin story instead of an invented word, and it ties the name to the single most
   recognizable asset you own.
2. **Two syllables, hard consonants.** Poundable, memorable, and easy to say in a sign-off.
3. **No language dependency.** Consistent with the locked global/mute-first mandate
   ([Stage 2](../docs/12-stage-2-channel-operating-system.md)) — it carries no meaning to
   mistranslate or mispronounce across markets.
4. **Short enough for a clean handle** and to sit large in a banner safe area.

### The double-P is the logo

`I-P-P-A`. The two central `P`s are **mirrored back-to-back** and read as the two leads facing each
other: one tinted `TEAL` (PIP's scarf), one tinted `BRAND_YELLOW` (CHIEF's medal sash). The name
*contains* the cast conflict. See [brand art specs](03-brand-art-specs.md).

### Tagline rationale

**"The jerk always gets it."** is lifted directly from the
[Brand Bible's Emotional Design section](../production/design/BRAND_BIBLE.md), which already
describes the intended days-later memory as *"the channel where the jerk always gets it."* Making
that the public tagline means the brand promise and the brand slogan are the same sentence.

It also passes the voice test: warm, playful, clever, brisk — and it is **not** clickbait, preachy,
or try-hard ([Brand Voice](../production/design/BRAND_BIBLE.md)).

Use the neutral alt (*Karma in 30 seconds*) where "jerk" is inappropriate — kids-adjacent surfaces,
sponsor decks, app-store-style listings.

## Backronyms — deliberately not used

`IPPA` could be forced into *Instant Payback, Perfectly Animated*. **Recommendation: don't.**
The [brand personality guardrails](../production/design/BRAND_BIBLE.md) rule out anything that reads
as try-hard, and a strained acronym is exactly that. Keep IPPA as a **name**, and let the
PIP-anagram be the story you tell when asked. If you ever want a descriptor, use the tagline.

## Handle candidates

YouTube handle rules ([official guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11585688)):
3–30 characters, must be unique, cannot be URL-like or phone-number-like, must follow Community
Guidelines. Each channel gets exactly one handle, and YouTube reserves the right to reclaim it.

Claim in this order — take the highest one that's free:

| Priority | Handle | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `@IPPA` | Ideal. A 4-character handle is very likely already taken. |
| 2 | `@IPPAtoons` | Best realistic target. Adds the category, stays short, reads clearly. |
| 3 | `@IPPAshorts` | Format-explicit; slightly weaker because it dates the channel to one format. |
| 4 | `@watchIPPA` | Verb-led, works well as a spoken CTA. |
| 5 | `@IPPAworld` | Leaves room for the multi-format expansion in [Future Expansion](../docs/32-future-expansion.md). |

**Avoid:** underscores and periods (hurt word-of-mouth and voice search), and any handle where the
`IPPA` stem isn't first — the stem must lead so it's the part people remember.

### ⚠️ Two things I could not verify for you

1. **Handle availability.** This has to be checked live in YouTube Studio at claim time.
2. **Name collisions.** `IPPA` is used as an acronym by various unrelated organisations and trade
   bodies. That is normally harmless for a differently-classed entertainment channel, but before you
   invest in merch or logo registration you should run a proper trademark search in your
   jurisdiction. **This is a legal question, not a branding one — get a professional opinion before
   spending money on it.** Also grab the matching handles on TikTok, Instagram, and X at the same
   time as YouTube, even if you don't post there yet.
