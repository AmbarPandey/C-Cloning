## Phase 4 — Bank the buffer (day 10–20)

Stage 2 mandates a **≥2-week buffer of approved ideas**. Go further and bank finished *videos* — a
missed upload breaks the "schedule is sacred" rule, and the sacred schedule is what the algorithm
rewards.

- [ ] ≥ 20 scored idea briefs approved (≥18/25 to proceed)
- [ ] **14 finished episodes** in hand, each through the 11-point Publish Gate
- [ ] Episode 1 = [A1 *The Wrong Scooter*](../production/A1-first-video/README.md)
- [ ] All 14 scheduled

> Launching with 14 banked means your first two weeks are guaranteed even if week one goes badly.
> Momentum matters far more than launching three days sooner.

## ⚠️ The YPP math problem

**Read this before you plan around the Day-90 monetization target.** There's a conflict between the
locked plan and how YPP eligibility actually works, and the repo docs don't currently address it.

**The two paths to full YPP ad revenue:**

| Path | Requirement |
|---|---|
| A | 1,000 subscribers + **4,000 valid public watch hours on long-form** in 365 days |
| B | 1,000 subscribers + **10 million valid public Shorts views** in 90 days |

**The problem:** watch time from Shorts in the Shorts feed does **not** count toward the long-form
4,000-hour threshold — the two paths are deliberately separate. So a Shorts-only channel
([D-05](../docs/21-decision-log.md)) has exactly one route: **path B, 10 million Shorts views in 90
days.**

At the locked cadence of 10–14/week you'll publish roughly 130–180 episodes in 90 days. Ten million
views across those means averaging **roughly 55,000–75,000 views per episode from a standing start**.
That is achievable for a channel that catches, but it is emphatically not a *plan* — it's an outcome.
Meanwhile [Objectives](../docs/02-objectives.md) states "YPP application submitted by ~Day 90" as a
success criterion.

*(Rough arithmetic on published thresholds, not a forecast.)*

**Three options. This is your call — it touches locked decision D-05, so it needs a Decision Log
entry either way:**

| Option | What it means | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Reframe Day 90** | Keep Shorts-only. Target the **500-subscriber fan-funding tier** by Day 90 and treat full YPP as a Day-180+ goal. | Honest and achievable. No strategy change. Slower to ad revenue. |
| **B. Add minimal long-form** | Amend D-05 to allow ~1 long-form upload/week — e.g. themed compilations of your own Shorts with new framing — to bank the 4,000 hours. | Opens the long-form path much earlier. **Caution:** low-effort compilations can run into reused-content policy. Needs genuine added value, and it partially re-opens D-05. |
| **C. Change nothing** | Keep the plan; accept that Day-90 YPP depends on a breakout. | Zero disruption. Highest chance of missing the stated criterion. |

**My read:** option **A**, with a Day-90 review gate that reconsiders B if 3-second retention is
consistently clearing 70% but raw view volume isn't scaling. A is honest about the timeline without
touching a locked decision, and the retention data you'll have by Day 90 is exactly what you need to
make the B call properly.

- [ ] Option chosen and recorded in the [Decision Log](../docs/21-decision-log.md)
- [ ] [Objectives](../docs/02-objectives.md) Day-90 criterion updated to match
