## Scene-by-scene

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF (0:00–0:01.5)
- **VIS:** ECU: CHIEF's face and cap brim, **already crouched and leaning down** toward the rim of a shallow ground-level stone trough, one glove braced on the ground. A desaturated duck silhouette occupies the frame edge, mid-blink. No park, no signpost, no context.
- **CAM:** Tight ECU, static, 45 frames. Motion underway on frame 1.
- **VO:** *"Take the short one."*
- **SFX:** No music. `SFX_trough_lap_v1` and one flat `SFX_duck_shuffle_v1`.

### C1b — GOAL DIAGRAM (0:01.5–0:02)
- **VIS:** Flat schematic of a signpost: two `INK` arrows from a common post. The upper arrow is **short** and ends in a small fountain pictogram **with a tiny paw symbol beside it**. The lower arrow is **long** and ends in a taller fountain pictogram. Wordless.
- **CAM:** Flat MED, dead centre, held 15 frames.
- **SFX:** Music **enters** on the cut. `SFX_ting_v1`.
- **Note (`TM3`):** the paw symbol is rendered at genuinely small scale. It must be *present and findable*, not *noticeable*. That gap is the entire replay mechanism.

### C2 — REWIND / SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** A flat park. The signpost stands centre. `POP_TEAL` foliage, desaturated animals lounging like people in the background.
- **ACT:** Both read the sign. PIP sets off down the long arrow's path without comment. CHIEF **taps the short arrow twice with a glove**, sneers at PIP's choice, and strides off.
- **CAM:** MED two-shot, slow push-in ending on the signpost — **the paw symbol is in frame and unemphasised.**
- **VO:** *"One of them read the whole sign."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_glove_tap_v1` ×2 · `SFX_footstep_v1` · `SFX_button_v1` "pfft"
- **EMO:** CHIEF `gloating` / PIP `neutral`
