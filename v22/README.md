**Story:** a park signpost offers two routes to the same fountain pictogram — one short, one long. CHIEF
takes the short one and arrives first. The pull-back reveals what the sign never hid: there are **two**
fountains, the short arrow led to the low animals' trough, and the tall one stands where PIP is now
drinking. The sign was honest. He read it fast.

**Seeds (`TM3`):** the two-arrow signpost, and the **tiny paw symbol** beside the short arrow's fountain —
in frame 1, legible only on rewatch.

## Mode B hypothesis (stated, as required)
> A twist delivered by **camera position** rather than by an event will beat the catalogue on **replay
> rate** and under-perform on **share rate**, because the viewer cannot verify the reveal without
> rewatching frame 1 to check the pictogram.
> **Success:** replay rate beats the v18–v21 average; share rate may fall below it.
> **Kill condition:** if replay rate does *not* beat the average, `TW6` is retired from Shorts and reserved
> for long form, where a reveal has room to be planted properly.

This is only the **second Mode B** in 22 episodes — the base audit found the catalogue roughly two
explorations short of the 1-in-5 ratio, and v22 plus v23 close that gap.

## What's new
- **First `NP7` Bait-and-Switch** and **first `TW6` Perspective Shift.** With v18–v21 this batch has now opened `NP5`, `NP7`, `NP8`, `TW6`, `TW7`, `TW9` and `TW10` — seven previously unused nodes against a catalogue where `NP3` alone held 53%.
- **The escalation inside the silence is *spatial, not dynamic*.** Three duck shuffles that get **closer**, not louder. Every prior episode escalated by volume or pitch; this one escalates by proximity.
- **The loudest sound belongs to PIP's side of frame.** In every other episode the peak happens *to* CHIEF. Here it's simply the correct fountain working properly, somewhere he didn't walk to — with his small trough lap still running underneath it.

## The integrity rule I want to call out
`NP7 Bait-and-Switch` is one keystroke away from being a **rug pull**, which the retention research names
as the highest-severity failure available — it damages channel trust, not just one video's retention. So
the constraint is written into all three files: **nothing is hidden by cheating, only by framing.** Both
fountains, both arrows and the paw symbol exist in the layout from frame 1 and never move. The sign is
truthful throughout; only the lens is close. If any element of the reveal were added late, this would stop
being a Perspective Shift and become the thing the research warns about.
