## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF + GOAL DIAGRAM (0:00-0:02)

> **Cold-open inversion.** C1 does not establish anything. It shows the ending already in motion, then
> hard-cuts to the drawn rule of the world. No character enters frame. Nothing is explained.

**C1a — the cold payoff (0:00-0:015)**
- **VIS:** Close on a fishing rod **snapping backward** under load, the overloaded line letting go — lures, weights and a fat bobber already airborne behind him in a spreading arc, the rod whipping bare. Cut at the top of the arc, gear still in the air.
- **CAM:** Tight. Subject fills the frame. Motion already underway on frame one — **no push-in from a wide, no entrance.**
- **HOOK TEXT (edit layer, not baked into the render):** *"He took the whole tackle box"*

**C1b — the goal diagram (0:015-0:02)**
- **VIS:** Hard cut to the pond bank: a shared tackle box open between two fishing spots, **one side crammed and one side empty**, and a `BRAND_YELLOW` arrow on the box lid pointing at the single item left in it — a bent pin on a length of string.
- **ACT:** PIP is on the bank in the same frame, already dropping his bent-pin-and-string into the water — same pond, no equipment.
- **CAM:** Hard cut. Static, medium-wide, held. This is the frame C8 returns to for the loop seam.
- **VO:** *"Every lure in the box. On one line."*
- **SFX:** Line singing under tension, then a sharp *crack*; hardware clattering in the air; a bare rod whistle; water lapping; bed enters on the diagram cut.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked · PIP neutral

**Load-bearing flaw planted here:** the line sags visibly under the load from frame 1. Loading every lure is what snaps it on the backswing.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium two-shot on the bank. CHIEF holds up his enormous loaded rod -- shiny reel glinting, one large metallic lure already dangling. He waves it mockingly at PIP. PIP calmly wraps his string three times around the stick tip, ties a knot, and the bent pin drops to dangle. CHIEF looks at PIP's stick, then at his own magnificent rod, and laughs openly -- pointing.
- **ACT:** CHIEF taunts with rod. PIP ties his line without looking up. CHIEF points and laughs.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in ending on the contrast between the two setups (elaborate rod left, bare stick right).
- **VO:** *"That was all that was left."*
- **SFX:** Reel *click-click* as CHIEF shows off; a soft string *whoosh* as PIP ties; CHIEF's mocking laugh; the pastoral bed builds gently.
- **EMO:** CHIEF mocking - PIP focused.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** Close-medium on CHIEF's line/rod. He clips on a **second lure** (shiny chrome, reflecting light). Then lifts a **third** (feathered, green and yellow). Then a **lead weight** (grey, heavy-looking). After each one he admires it, holding it up to the light, then clips it onto the line. The line **visibly sags lower** with each addition -- the tip of the rod dips. Meanwhile, in the right of frame, PIP has already cast: his stick is propped on a small rock, and the bent pin bobs gently in the water at the end of its taut string.
- **ACT:** CHIEF clips, admires, clips, admires, clips. The line droops more each time. PIP sits beside his stick, hands in his scarf, watching the water.
- **CAM:** Static close-medium; 4-frame hold after each new attachment to register the additional sag.
- **VO:** *"One more. Just one more."*
- **SFX:** Metallic *click* for each clip-on; a soft *plink* as PIP's pin enters water; the bed adds a plucky banjo layer (one strum per attachment, like a countdown).
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating - PIP patient.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** The overloading continues and gets absurd. CHIEF clips on: a **massive red bobber** (the size of his fist -- `ALERT_RED`, impossible to miss), then **two more lead weights**, then a **fourth lure** (the fanciest -- a multi-jointed metallic fish shape). The line now **sags almost to the ground** -- a heavy catenary curve between the rod tip and the cluster of gear. The rod tip itself bends downward under the static weight. CHIEF never looks at the line -- he is too busy polishing his reel housing and flexing his medals in the reflection. PIP's stick-line gives a small **twitch** (a nibble -- the string tenses for 2 frames then relaxes).
- **ACT:** CHIEF adds, admires reel, flexes. The sag deepens. PIP notices the twitch but doesn't react beyond a small head-tilt.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in (100% to 105%) framing the full sag of the line prominently. 3-frame hold on each new attachment. The rod tip bending is clearly visible.
- **VO:** *"Perfect. Absolutely perfect."*
- **SFX:** Heavier *clonk* for each weight; the line itself makes a faint stressed *creak*; a tiny water *plink* (the nibble on PIP's line); the bed reaches its brightest, densest point with an added fiddle trill.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant (oblivious) - PIP patient.

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** CHIEF plants his feet wide on the bank (a power stance). He grips the rod with both hands. He winds up for a CAST -- rearing back with full theatrical force. The overloaded line swings behind him in a heavy arc -- all four lures, three weights, and the massive bobber swinging in a cluster. The line pulls **taut at maximum tension** between the rod tip and the gear cluster, stretched in a diagonal behind his head. **Music cuts to silence.** A single held beat: the line vibrating visibly, the rod tip bent backward, everything at breaking point.
- **ACT:** One enormous theatrical wind-up held at maximum extension. The line at peak tension. Time seems to stop.
- **CAM:** Low angle looking up at CHIEF's silhouette and the line stretched against the `SKY`-blue sky. Static hold on the taut line vibrating.
- **VO:** *"And then--"* (cuts off with the music)
- **SFX:** The bed swells to a brief crescendo, then **CUTS TO SILENCE** on the frame the line reaches full tension; only the faint *vibration hum* of the stretched line remains -- the highest-pitched, thinnest sound possible. One water lap underneath.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant (L3).

### C6 - PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** In silence: the line **SNAPS** at the rod tip. A clean break. The freed cluster of gear -- four lures, three weights, massive bobber -- launches **backward** over CHIEF's head (following through from the backswing) and **SPLASHES** into the pond BEHIND him (the completely wrong direction). A large splash. Meanwhile, the rod whips forward (now with nothing attached but the bare mainline ending in a single empty hook). The hook travels in a sad little arc and lands in the water in front of CHIEF with a pathetic, tiny *ploop*. CHIEF holds his follow-through pose, rod extended forward, and slowly looks down at the bare line dangling in the water. Then slowly turns to look behind him at the spreading ripples where his gear sank. The big red bobber surfaces for one second, then sinks.
- **ACT:** Snap -> gear launches backward -> splash behind -> rod whips forward bare -> tiny ploop in front -> CHIEF frozen -> slow realization turn.
- **CAM:** Wide shot covering both the pond behind (splash) and the water in front (ploop). 4-frame hold on the snap moment, then let the physics play out. 0.5 s hold on CHIEF's frozen follow-through pose before he turns.
- **VO:** (silence) optional whisper: *"...snap."*
- **SFX:** The **SNAP** (sharp, musical, like a guitar string breaking -- the loudest sound in the silence); the heavy **SPLASH** behind (large, with multiple plops from individual items hitting water); the tiny pathetic *ploop* in front (the contrast that sells it); one bubble *blip* as the bobber resurfaces then sinks; silence.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked - PIP not visible (off-frame).

### C7 - TWIST / INSTANT KARMA (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** PIP's stick **twitches**. His string goes taut. He lifts the stick with one hand -- a small, cheerful **fish** dangles from the bent pin, wriggling happily. PIP smiles (a small, genuine smile). He holds the fish up -- it is tiny, modest, real. Beside him in frame: CHIEF still holds his bare rod over the empty water in front of him. His single naked hook sits in the water, catching nothing. The empty rod, the empty water, the empty reel. PIP with a fish on a stick vs. CHIEF with nothing on a fortune.
- **ACT:** PIP lifts fish. The contrast holds. CHIEF's expression runs: shocked -> dejected -> defeated.
- **CAM:** Medium wide framing both characters. Quick punch-in on the wriggling fish, then settle wide on the contrast. **0.5 s freeze on PIP's fish vs. CHIEF's empty hook.**
- **VO:** *"PIP used a stick."*
- **SFX:** Music **SLAMS back** with a bright fiddle stinger; a cheerful *splish* as the fish emerges; the fish's wiggle has a tiny *flap-flap*; record-scratch on CHIEF's frozen face.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP gleeful. **<-- the SHARE trigger.**

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. The same pond, the same bank. CHIEF slumped on the dock, bare rod drooping in his lap, the empty tackle box open beside him (mirror of C1 but now HIS setup is the empty one). PIP stands beside him, **holding out the small fish** toward CHIEF -- offering to share, no gloating, no smirk. PIP turns and waves at camera. In the background water, a single **bubble** rises where CHIEF's gear sank (a callback).
- **ACT:** PIP offers the fish. CHIEF takes it gingerly with one glove. PIP waves. One bubble pops in the background.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing (wide, static); hard cut out within 1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** A gentle water *bloop* (the background bubble); warm resolve chord; button *ding*; the pond lapping continues into the seam.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP kind. **Loop seam:** C8 uses the same pond-and-bank composition as C1 -- only the tackle state, CHIEF's expression, and PIP's catch differ, so the replay re-reads the stick-and-string and the sag with the ending in mind.
