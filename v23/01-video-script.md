## Scene-by-scene

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF (0:00–0:01.5)
- **VIS:** ECU on an oversized `PAPER` glove's thumb **already pressing down** into a dusting of powder on a dark surface, lifting to leave a crisp tread mark. No room, no face.
- **CAM:** Tight ECU, static, 45 frames. Motion underway on frame 1.
- **VO:** *"Somebody took the jar."*
- **SFX:** No music. `SFX_powder_press_v1`.

### C1b — GOAL DIAGRAM (0:01.5–0:02)
- **VIS:** Flat schematic comparison card: a **tread pattern** on the left, an `INK` equals sign, a generic culprit silhouette on the right; a `POP_TEAL` **tick** and an `ALERT_RED` **cross** beneath as the two possible verdicts. Wordless.
- **CAM:** Flat MED, dead centre, held 15 frames.
- **SFX:** Music **enters**. `SFX_ting_v1`.
- **Note (`TM3`):** the tread pattern on the card is drawn **boldly and specifically** — it must be memorable enough to re-match on rewatch. This is the one seed in the batch that is *loud* by design; the concealment is that nobody thinks to compare it to a glove.

### C2 — REWIND / SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** A plain storeroom shelf with a clean **ring** where a jar stood. CHIEF taps out a pinch of powder.
- **ACT:** He dusts, `gloating`. **The tread on his `PAPER` glove is plainly visible** in the same frame as the card (**SEED B**, unremarked). PIP begins checking the shelf's underside.
- **CAM:** MED two-shot, slow push-in ending with glove and card **both in frame**.
- **VO:** *"One of them looked for the jar."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_powder_tap_v1` · `SFX_glove_creak_v1` · `SFX_button_v1` "pfft"
- **EMO:** CHIEF `gloating` / PIP `neutral`

### C3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:06–0:11)
- **VIS:** More powder. He locates a print, raises the card beside it, compares — `gloating`, delighted.
- **ACT:** PIP, undramatic, is on hands and knees looking *under* the shelf.
- **CAM:** Static; 4-frame hold on the card-beside-print comparison.
- **VO:** *"The other one looked for a culprit."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_powder_puff_v1` ×2 · `SFX_card_flick_v1`
- **EMO:** CHIEF `gloating` / PIP `determined`

### C4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:11–0:16)
- **VIS:** Powder everywhere — a whiteout across shelf, floor and jars. **Every surface he has braced, leaned on or steadied now carries his tread.**
- **ACT:** He works faster, finding more prints, each one confirming him further. He never looks at his hands.
- **CAM:** Slight push-in; 3-frame hold on each new print appearing where he just touched.
- **VO:** *"The scene was full of evidence."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_powder_puff_v1` ×3 rising · `SFX_glove_creak_v1` · `SFX_button_v1` fanfare stab
- **EMO:** CHIEF `triumphant` / PIP `determined`

### C5 — FULL COMMIT (0:16–0:22)
- **VIS:** He holds the comparison card and a lifted print **together overhead** in a triumphant pose, `FX_sparkle_v1`. The two patterns visibly agree. **He has solved it — completely and officially.**
- **ACT:** Long held tableau, ~1.5 s freeze. Sell the solve entirely.
- **CAM:** Low hero angle, push-in settling into the hold. **Framing excludes his other glove.**
- **VO:** *"A perfect match."* → stop.
- **SFX:** Bed peaks, **CUTS TO SILENCE mid-phrase**. Only `SFX_powder_settle_v1`.
- **EMO:** CHIEF `triumphant` (L3)

### C6 — PATTERN BREAK (0:22–0:27)
- **VIS:** In silence the powder settles. The frame holds **three treads at once**: on the card, on the shelf edge, and on the glove holding the card. Identical.
- **ACT:** Three discrete settle beats. His grin holds, then falters as his eye travels down his own arm.
- **CAM:** Static, then slow tilt down from card to glove.
- **VO:** (silence) optional whisper *"…his own."*
- **SFX:** Silence + `SFX_powder_settle_v1` ×3 · one `SFX_glove_creak_v1`
- **EMO:** CHIEF `smug` → `confused` → `worried`

### C7 — TWIST · HIDDEN CAUSE (0:27–0:31)
- **VIS:** He turns his glove over. **The card matches his own tread exactly.** Pull wide: every print in the whiteout is his. Behind him, PIP calmly **lifts the missing jar out from under the shelf** — it was never taken; it rolled.
- **ACT:** `triumphant` → `shocked` → `panicked`, looking card → glove → room. Cap, sash, medals all on, powder-dusted.
- **CAM:** Punch-in on the glove-to-card match, settle wide; **0.5 s freeze** on CHIEF holding evidence against himself while PIP holds the jar.
- **VO:** *"Nobody took the jar."*
- **SFX:** Music **SLAMS back** · `SFX_glove_creak_v1` (loud) · `SFX_jar_scrape_v1` · `SFX_scratch_v1`
- **EMO:** CHIEF `panicked` / PIP `gleeful`

### C8 — PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31–0:32)
- **VIS:** CHIEF holds a card that convicts him, `sheepish`, powder-dusted. PIP **sets the jar back into its clean ring** on the shelf — plainly, no gloating. Frame settles to the exact C1b card composition.
- **CAM:** Wide, then settle to C1b framing as the final frame.
- **VO:** *"Every time."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_jar_set_v1` · warm resolve · `SFX_button_v1` ding
- **EMO:** CHIEF `sheepish` / PIP `relieved`
