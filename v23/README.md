**Story:** a jar has gone from a shelf. CHIEF dusts for glove-prints, escalating from a pinch of powder to
a whiteout, and triumphantly matches a print to his comparison card. The print is **his own glove tread** —
the pattern is on his gloves in frame 1, and every surface he touched while investigating now carries it.
Nobody took the jar. It rolled under the shelf. He just printed the entire room.

**Seeds (`TM3`):** the tread pattern on the card, and the **same tread on his own glove** at 0:03.

## Mode B hypothesis (stated, as required)
> A `TW5 Hidden Cause` seed placed **on the antagonist's own body** will beat v5's *environmental* hidden
> cause on **replay rate**, because the evidence is attached to a character the viewer is already watching
> rather than to a prop in the background.
> **Success:** replay rate beats v5's. **Kill condition:** if it doesn't, hidden-cause seeds revert to
> environmental and `TW5` stays a long-form container only.

With v22 this is the **third Mode B in 23 episodes**, which brings the catalogue back to roughly the
1-in-5 ratio the base audit found it short of.

## What's new
- **Second `NP5`** in the batch (a different, fresh pair), and `SC6`'s **first appearance as a Short**.
- **The seed is loud, and hidden anyway.** Every other seed in the series is concealed by being small or occluded. This one is bold and specific by design — the concealment is that nobody thinks to compare the card to the investigator's own hand. It's the only seed in the batch that hides in plain sight.
- **A descending countdown.** Inside the silence the powder settles in three beats that get *quieter* (-18 → -22 → -26 dB), not louder. It's the only inverted escalation in the series: the room going still is what finally lets him hear his own glove.
- **Two signatures at once.** Powder escalates `tap → puff → press → SETTLE` and then fades to nothing; underneath it a glove creak has been running since 0:03 at -19 dB, escalated only by **unmasking**, never by performance. One recording, four levels.

## Distinct from v5 — deliberately checked
v5 shares `SC6` + `TW5` + `TM3`, so I verified the difference rather than assuming it: **v5 is an
investigation of a real event** with an environmental cause (a ledger tipped a table). **v23 is an
investigation that is itself the disaster**, with the cause on the investigator's own body. Different
pattern (`NP5` vs `NP4`), different mechanic (`CM-B1` vs `CM-A2`), opposite structure — v5 clears suspects,
v23 manufactures evidence.

## Honest note on the score
`NP5 ↔ SC6` is marked **4/5**, not 5/5. Crime scenes host *investigation* patterns more naturally than
*disaster escalation*; making `NP5` fit requires the investigation itself to be the disaster, which works
but is a slightly forced join. Three of the four nodes are Medium-confidence tiers. **6.0 is the truthful
output** and I didn't round the edge up to flatter it.
