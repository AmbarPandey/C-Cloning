# v2 — "The Victory Lap" (production package)

A complete, cross-synced YouTube Shorts package for video **v2**, built from the locked A2 idea
(NP2 Underdog Reversal · SC3 Competition · CM-E1 Escalation · TW1 Role Reversal · FinalScore 9.0).
Cast: **CHIEF** (self-declared champion) vs **PIP** (tiny underdog challenger). ~32 s, 9:16,
mute-first with an optional VO layer.

All 5 files below lock to the **same master timeline** (defined in `01-video-script.md`), so the
render, narration, and audio drop straight onto shared timecodes — designed for minimal editing.
The timeline uses the **same 8-clip / 32-second / 11-second-silence skeleton as v1**, so the series
stays formulaic and repeatable.

| # | File | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`01-video-script.md`](01-video-script.md) | The beat sheet + scene-by-scene script + master timeline |
| 2 | [`02-image-generation-reference.md`](02-image-generation-reference.md) | Characters, reactions, surroundings, props + a paste-ready image prompt per shot |
| 3 | [`03-voiceover-script.md`](03-voiceover-script.md) | Timed narrator storytelling script (amplifier layer) |
| 4 | [`04-video-generation-prompt.md`](04-video-generation-prompt.md) | Full video-gen prompt: motion, camera angles, transitions, cut/freeze timing, FX per shot |
| 5 | [`05-audio-bgm-sfx-reference.md`](05-audio-bgm-sfx-reference.md) | BGM, SFX, the silence beat + a master sync map of all layers |

**Story in one line:** a boastful champion straps on rocket after rocket to win by a humiliating
margin, and flies so high he sails clean *over* the low finish tape without ever breaking it — while
the tiny challenger rolls up and snaps it with his chest. The roles swap completely.

**The seeds:** the finish tape is strung *low* (visible in frame 1) — his own escalation lifts him
above it; and the drab participation ribbon he contemptuously flicks away in the setup ends up pinned
to his own chest.

Reusable, series-wide style/character vocabulary lives in the repo-root
[`IMAGE-GEN-REFERENCE.md`](../IMAGE-GEN-REFERENCE.md). Previous episode: [`../v1`](../v1).
