## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend — **VIS** visual/staging · **ACT** action · **CAM** camera · **VO** narration (optional layer) · **SFX** sound · **EMO** emotion

### C1 — HOOK + SEED (0:00–0:02)
- **VIS:** Wide flat race track. `ASPHALT` lane, `SKY` backdrop, flat **empty** grandstand shapes + bunting in bg. Checkered start line foreground-left. Far end mid-frame: the **checkered finish tape strung LOW**, at PIP's chest height (**the seed**), beside a small podium with a drab `ASPHALT`-grey **participation ribbon** pinned to it (the callback seed).
- **ACT:** CHIEF struts in from left, chest out, wheeling a rocket scooter, a giant `BRAND_YELLOW` trophy tucked under one arm. PIP waits at the line on tiny roller skates.
- **CAM:** Static wide establishing (this exact framing returns in C8).
- **VO:** *"The champion brought a rocket."*
- **SFX:** Upbeat race bed (low); a "boing" per strut step; small engine idle.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug · PIP worried.

### C2 — SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium two-shot at the start line.
- **ACT:** CHIEF plants the giant trophy on the podium as if already won, **flicks the little participation ribbon** off it so it flutters down at PIP's skates, then points at him mockingly.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in on the smug point.
- **VO:** *"The challenger brought roller skates."*
- **SFX:** Trophy clunk; dismissive "pfft" flick; comedic "aha" sting.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating · PIP worried.

### C3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:06–0:11)
- **VIS:** The checkered start flag drops.
- **ACT:** Booster #1 ignites — CHIEF launches down the track as a flat smear of motion lines. PIP begins a slow, determined roll.
- **CAM:** Static wide; brief whip-follow of the launch, then settle.
- **VO:** *"Nobody was betting on the skates."*
- **SFX:** Flag whoosh; **booster ignition #1** (the signature sound); doppler pass-by; tiny skate squeaks.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant · PIP determined.

### C4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:11–0:16)
- **VIS:** CHIEF is already far ahead — but not satisfied.
- **ACT:** He straps on boosters **#2, #3, #4** in comic pop-ins. The engine scream rises; his wheels lift off the ground.
- **CAM:** Slight push-in; 3-frame hold on each booster pop-in.
- **VO:** *"But winning wasn't enough. He wanted a margin."*
- **SFX:** **Booster ignitions #2–#4, each a step higher in pitch**; rising engine scream; proud fanfare stab.
- **EMO:** CHIEF peak-gloat · PIP determined (still rolling).
- **Note:** this is the fatal motive — his greed for a bigger margin is what lifts him over the tape.

### C5 — ANTICIPATION (0:16–0:22)
- **VIS:** Absurd velocity; flat ghost-smears trail him. The scooter's nose tips upward — he is now genuinely airborne, sparkles trailing, chest out, aimed dead at the finish. He does not level out.
- **ACT:** Long proud hold at the peak of the climb.
- **CAM:** Low hero-angle rise/push-in that settles into the hold.
- **VO:** *"So he went faster."* → then silence.
- **SFX:** Bed swells, then **CUTS TO SILENCE** (the pattern-break begins); only a thin distant engine whine.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant.

### C6 — PATTERN BREAK (0:22–0:27)
- **VIS:** In near-silence he sails **clean over the low tape** — it is never touched. The tape sways gently, intact. He shrinks toward the horizon, still locked in his victory pose, oblivious. Far behind, PIP keeps rolling and looks up with dawning hope.
- **ACT:** The tape's stillness is the joke — hold on it.
- **CAM:** Static wide, deep flat focus; suspense hold on the untouched tape.
- **VO:** (silence — let it breathe) optional whisper *"…all of it."*
- **SFX:** Silence, broken only by a fading doppler whine, one suspense "tick," and a soft flutter of the tape.
- **EMO:** CHIEF oblivious · PIP hopeful.

### C7 — TWIST / ROLE REVERSAL (0:27–0:31)
- **VIS:** PIP rolls up and **breaks the finish tape with his chest.** Confetti bursts; the checkered flag waves. Cut to PIP up on the podium, the giant `BRAND_YELLOW` trophy in his arms, the champion medal round his neck.
- **ACT:** In the distance CHIEF slams to a stop, whips around, and sees it — face snapping **smug → shocked → panicked**. PIP beams.
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in on the tape snap; hard cut to the podium; ~0.5 s freeze on PIP + trophy.
- **VO:** *"He forgot one thing. You have to cross the line."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; tape SNAP; confetti pop; medal ding; long skid screech; record-scratch.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked · PIP gleeful. **← the SHARE trigger.**

### C8 — PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31–0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1 — but the roles are swapped: **PIP is on the podium** with the trophy; **CHIEF stands deflated in PIP's old start-line spot**, boosters spent and drooping, still wearing his cap and sash.
- **ACT:** PIP leans down and pins the drab **participation ribbon** on CHIEF's chest (the callback pays off), then gives a small wave to camera.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out ≤1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; also seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** Sad booster deflate "pffffft"; ribbon pin tap; warm resolve chord; button "ding."
- **EMO:** CHIEF sheepish · PIP relieved. **Loop seam:** C8 reuses C1's exact framing, background plate and horizon — only the cast and their status props are swapped, so the replay re-reads the opening with the reversal in mind.
