## Thumbnail brief
- **Grab a frame** between Shot 5 and Shot 7: CHIEF mid-victory-pose with the tow hook just entering behind him (tension, not a full spoiler).
- **Layout:** CHIEF big on the left (smug face), tow-truck hook peeking top-right, PIP tiny bottom-right.
- **Optional text (≤3 words):** `WRONG MOVE` in black on a brand-yellow pill, top area, not covering faces.
- **Keep** the red no-parking zone visible as a subtle clue.
- **Export:** one 1080×1920 vertical still, plus a 1280×720 center crop for the channel grid.
