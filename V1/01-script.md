# V1 — Cinematic Script

**Title (working):** *Wrong Scooter* · **Runtime:** ~32 s · **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) ·
**Frame rate:** 30 fps · **Dialogue:** none (mute-first) · **Tone:** playful, satisfying, advertiser-safe.

## Logline
A pompous parking officer (**CHIEF**) gleefully boots and tickets a tiny, innocent underdog (**PIP**).
At the peak of his victory pose, a tow truck hauls away **CHIEF's own** scooter — the one he parked
in the no-parking zone in the very first frame — using **CHIEF's own boot**. Instant karma.

## Story structure (why it works)
- **Hook (0–2s):** conflict + a hidden clue (CHIEF's scooter in the red no-parking zone).
- **Escalation (2–16s):** CHIEF abuses power; the audience roots against him.
- **Silence + anticipation (16–27s):** music drops; you *feel* something coming.
- **Twist (27–31s):** karma lands on CHIEF, not PIP. → the "share" moment.
- **Button + loop (31–32s):** PIP's calm wave; last frame = first frame (seamless replay).

## Visual style (applies to every shot)
Flat-color 2D cartoon · thick uniform black outlines · no gradients · minimal single-tone shading ·
chunky proportions · high contrast · clean and mobile-legible.
**Palette:** ink black `#1A1A1A` · brand yellow `#FFD400` · warm paper `#FFF7E0` · sky blue `#BFE3F2` ·
asphalt grey `#6E7076` · alert red `#E4322B` · teal `#2FB6A3`.

## Shot list (the 8 frames)

> Each shot = one scene image + one short motion clip. Legend: **FRAME** what's on screen ·
> **CAMERA** framing/move · **LIGHT** lighting mood · **ACTION** what moves · **BEAT** story purpose.

### Shot 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:02)
- **FRAME:** Wide parking lot. Asphalt ground, blue sky. Left: a parking meter where tiny **PIP** fumbles coins. Center: **CHIEF** strutting in, chest out, smug. **Lower-right foreground: a bold red "NO PARKING" zone with CHIEF's scooter parked inside it** (the clue — visible but not emphasized).
- **CAMERA:** Locked wide establishing shot. No move. Hold the full composition ~0.5s so the clue registers.
- **LIGHT:** Bright, flat midday. Even, cheerful.
- **ACTION:** CHIEF strut-walk cycle; PIP nervous coin fumble.
- **BEAT:** Establish authority vs innocence; plant the seed.

### Shot 2 — SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **FRAME:** Medium two-shot. CHIEF spots PIP's front tire a hair over the line. He grins and pulls out an oversized ticket pad and a giant rubber stamp.
- **CAMERA:** Slow push-in toward CHIEF's grin.
- **LIGHT:** Same bright flat light; tiny glint on the stamp.
- **ACTION:** CHIEF "draws" the stamp like a weapon; PIP shrinks.
- **BEAT:** The bully targets the underdog.

### Shot 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:06–0:11)
- **FRAME:** CHIEF slaps a ticket on PIP's tiny scooter, then clamps a bright **yellow wheel boot** on it with a flourish. PIP's eyes well up.
- **CAMERA:** Static medium; tiny impact shake on the stamp hit.
- **LIGHT:** Flat; a small comedic "spotlight" pop on the boot.
- **ACTION:** Stamp slam → boot clamp → CHIEF blows on the stamp like a gunslinger.
- **BEAT:** Injustice; audience anger builds.

### Shot 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:11–0:16)
- **FRAME:** A comical mountain of tickets piled on PIP's scooter. CHIEF polishes his medals, admiring himself.
- **CAMERA:** Slight push-in; slow.
- **LIGHT:** Flat; sparkle glint on medals.
- **ACTION:** Rapid ticket "pop-ins" stacking up; CHIEF medal-polish loop.
- **BEAT:** Peak injustice; CHIEF's overconfidence maxes out.

### Shot 5 — ANTICIPATION (0:16–0:22)
- **FRAME:** CHIEF steps onto a tiny podium, plants a little flag, strikes an over-the-top **victory pose**, stamp raised triumphantly to the sky.
- **CAMERA:** Low **hero angle** looking up at CHIEF; slow rise.
- **LIGHT:** Slightly dramatic — a soft radial glow behind him, sparkles.
- **ACTION:** Snap into the hero pose; hold ~1.5s. **Music cuts to silence here.**
- **BEAT:** Maximum ego → the audience predicts a fall.

### Shot 6 — PATTERN BREAK (0:22–0:27)
- **FRAME:** Wide with depth. CHIEF frozen mid-pose, foreground-center, oblivious. Behind him, a **tow truck** rolls in; its hook swings — **past** PIP's scooter — toward the red no-parking corner.
- **CAMERA:** Locked wide, deep focus. Very slight zoom toward the truck hook.
- **LIGHT:** A subtle cool shadow creeps in as the truck arrives (tension).
- **ACTION:** Truck drives in; hook aligns with CHIEF's scooter. PIP notices, eyes widening with hope.
- **BEAT:** Withhold the resolution; misdirection.

### Shot 7 — TWIST / INSTANT KARMA (0:27–0:31)
- **FRAME:** Punch-in wide. The tow hook clamps **CHIEF's own scooter** in the no-parking zone; the truck's arm grabs **CHIEF's own giant stamp** and slams a huge red **"TOWED"** mark on it. CHIEF is yanked off the podium, face snapping smug → shocked → panicked.
- **CAMERA:** Quick **punch-in**, then settle. Small screen shake on the stamp impact.
- **LIGHT:** Bright comedic "impact" flash / star-burst behind the stamp.
- **ACTION:** Hook clamp → stamp "TOWED" slam → CHIEF flails. PIP gleeful. **Music slams back.**
- **BEAT:** The payoff. This is the moment people share.

### Shot 8 — BUTTON + LOOP (0:31–0:32)
- **FRAME:** Wide composition **identical to Shot 1**. CHIEF shrinks into the distance, hanging off his towed scooter, medals jangling. PIP center-frame calmly peels the boot off his own scooter (it pops free) and gives a small wave to camera.
- **CAMERA:** Return to the exact Shot-1 framing (for the seamless loop).
- **LIGHT:** Back to bright, cheerful flat light (matches Shot 1).
- **ACTION:** Boot pop-off; PIP wave; CHIEF shrinks away. Hard cut on the wave.
- **BEAT:** Justice + a likeable close; the matching frame invites an instant replay.

## Continuity rules (must-follow)
1. **CHIEF's scooter stays in the same screen spot** (lower-right, in the red zone) in Shots 1, 6, 7.
2. **Shot 8 framing = Shot 1 framing** exactly (same camera, same background) so the video loops seamlessly.
3. The **boot** and **stamp** are CHIEF's tools in Shots 2–5, then used **against him** in Shot 7 — keep them identical.
4. Everything must be **clear with the sound off** (mute-first). Emotions read from pose + face, not words.
5. On-screen text appears **only** on the "TOWED" stamp in Shot 7.

## Editing notes
- **Hard cuts only** (no fades). One **punch-in** (Shot 7) and one **freeze** (~0.5s hold on the "TOWED" frame for screenshots).
- Keep total length **25–40s** (target ~32s). Hard cut out right after PIP's wave.
