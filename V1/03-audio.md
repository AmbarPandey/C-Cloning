# V1 — Audio (Voice-over, Music, Sound Effects)

V1 is **mute-first**: it must be fully understandable with the sound OFF. Audio only *amplifies* the
comedy. There is **no voice-over/dialogue** in V1 (this maximizes global reach — no language barrier).

## 1. Voice-over / dialogue
- **None for V1.** Do not generate any spoken lines.
- *(For future videos that need a voice, clone one fixed voice per character once and reuse it so the
  characters always sound the same. Keep any lines short and visual-led.)*

## 2. Background music (BGM)
One playful comedy track with a deliberate silent gap. Style: light, bouncy, cartoonish, upbeat,
royalty-free / owned (no copyrighted music).

| Time | Music behavior |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:16 | Light, jaunty comedy bed; gently **builds** as CHIEF escalates |
| 0:16–0:27 | **SILENCE** — cut the music at CHIEF's victory pose (this creates tension and holds viewers) |
| 0:27–0:31 | Music **slams back** hard on the twist (big comedic hit) |
| 0:31–0:32 | Warm, satisfying resolve chord on PIP's wave |

> The **11-second silence (0:16–0:27)** is the single most important audio choice — it signals
> "something is coming" and pulls viewers to the twist. Only a faint truck rumble breaks it.

## 4. Loudness / delivery
- Mix so nothing is needed to understand the story (mute-first).
- Target overall loudness around -14 LUFS, peaks under -1 dB (comfortable for phone speakers).

## Quick audio build order
1. Lay the music bed (0:00–0:16), then cut to silence (0:16–0:27), then slam back (0:27–0:32).
2. Drop each SFX at the timestamp above.
3. Play it **with the picture** and confirm the twist hits exactly when the music returns (~0:28).
