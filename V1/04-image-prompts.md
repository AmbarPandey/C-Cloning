# V1 — Scene Image Prompts (one per shot)

Paste each prompt into your image tool to create the **8 scene stills**. Always attach your saved
**PIP** and **CHIEF** reference sheets (from [02-characters-and-reactions.md](02-characters-and-reactions.md))
so the characters stay identical.

## Style prefix (already baked into each prompt below)
Flat-color 2D cartoon, thick black outlines, no gradients, minimal shading, 9:16 vertical, high
contrast, mobile-legible. Palette: ink `#1A1A1A`, brand yellow `#FFD400`, paper `#FFF7E0`, sky
`#BFE3F2`, asphalt `#6E7076`, alert red `#E4322B`, teal `#2FB6A3`. Advertiser-safe, no text unless stated.

---

**Shot 1 — Hook**
```
Flat-2D cartoon, 9:16 vertical, thick black outlines, no gradients. Wide parking lot: asphalt-grey
ground, sky-blue top. Left: a parking meter with tiny PIP (paper-white body, teal scarf, big worried
eyes) fumbling coins. Center: pompous officer CHIEF (teal jacket, peaked cap, yellow medal sash, smug)
strutting in, chest out. Lower-right foreground: an alert-red "NO PARKING" painted zone with CHIEF's
teal-and-yellow scooter parked inside it. Balanced, calm composition. No text. Palette locked.
```

**Shot 2 — Setup**
```
{style} Medium two-shot. CHIEF grinning, pulling out an oversized ticket pad and a giant yellow-black
rubber stamp, pointing at PIP's tiny scooter whose front tire is just over a white line. PIP shrinking,
worried. Bright flat light, glint on the stamp. No text.
```

**Shot 3 — Escalation 1**
```
{style} CHIEF slapping a paper ticket on PIP's tiny teal scooter and clamping a bright yellow wheel
boot on its wheel with a flourish, blowing on the giant stamp like a gunslinger. PIP teary, big glossy
eyes. Small comedic spotlight pop on the yellow boot. Motion lines on the stamp. No text.
```

**Shot 4 — Escalation 2**
```
{style} Medium-wide. A comical tall mountain of paper tickets piled on PIP's little scooter. CHIEF
standing proud beside it, polishing his yellow medals, chin up, gloating. PIP slumped, defeated.
Sparkle glints on the medals. No text.
```

**Shot 5 — Anticipation (victory pose)**
```
{style} Low hero camera angle looking up at CHIEF, who stands on a small podium with a tiny officer
flag, striking an over-the-top victory pose, the giant stamp raised triumphantly to the sky. Soft
radial glow and sparkles behind him. Dramatic empty space around him. Proud, ego-maximum. No text.
```

**Shot 6 — Pattern break**
```
{style} Wide shot with depth. CHIEF frozen mid victory-pose in the foreground-center, oblivious. In
the background-right, a friendly yellow-grey tow truck rolls in, its hook arm swinging toward the
alert-red NO-PARKING zone where CHIEF's scooter sits (NOT toward PIP's scooter). PIP off to the side,
eyes widening with hope. Slight cool shadow creeping in for tension. No text.
```

**Shot 7 — Twist / instant karma**
```
{style} Punch-in wide. The tow truck's hook clamps CHIEF'S OWN teal-yellow scooter in the red
NO-PARKING zone; the truck's arm wields CHIEF's own giant stamp to slam a huge alert-red "TOWED" mark
onto his scooter. CHIEF yanked off the podium mid-air, face shocked/panicked, arms flailing, cap
tilting. PIP gleeful. Big comedic star-burst impact flash behind the stamp. Only on-frame text: the
word "TOWED".
```

**Shot 8 — Button + loop (must match Shot 1 framing)**
```
{style} Wide composition IDENTICAL to Shot 1 (same camera, same parking-lot background, same light).
CHIEF shrinking into the distance on the right, hanging off his towed scooter, medals jangling. PIP
center-frame, calmly peeling the popped-off yellow boot from his scooter, giving a small friendly wave
to camera, relieved smile. Bright cheerful flat light. No text.
```

## Tips
- Generate Shot 1 and Shot 8 as a **matched pair** (same background/framing) for the loop.
- Keep CHIEF's scooter in the **same lower-right red zone** in Shots 1, 6, 7.
- If a character drifts off-model, regenerate with the reference image attached.
