# V1 — Image→Video Motion Prompts (one per shot)

For each shot, feed the **scene still** (from [04-image-prompts.md](04-image-prompts.md)) plus the
motion prompt below into your image→video tool (e.g. Kling, Runway, Luma, Pika, Veo, Sora). Keep each
clip the length shown. Generate at 9:16, 30 fps.

> General settings for every clip: *2D cartoon animation, snappy pose-to-pose motion, minimal camera
> movement, keep the character design and colors identical to the input image, no morphing, no extra
> characters, no text overlays (except where the image already has "TOWED").*

| Shot | Clip length | Motion prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~2s | CHIEF strut-walks in from the left with a proud bounce; PIP fumbles coins at the meter. Camera locked. Everything else still. Hold the wide composition. |
| 2 | ~4s | CHIEF grins and whips out the ticket pad and giant stamp with a flourish; PIP flinches and shrinks back slightly. Very slow push-in toward CHIEF. |
| 3 | ~5s | CHIEF slams the stamp down (small impact shake), then clamps the yellow boot onto PIP's scooter wheel; PIP's eyes well up. CHIEF blows on the stamp. Camera static. |
| 4 | ~5s | Paper tickets rapidly pop onto the pile one by one; CHIEF proudly polishes his medals in a loop, chin high. Very slow push-in. |
| 5 | ~6s | CHIEF snaps into a triumphant victory pose on the podium and holds it, stamp raised, sparkles twinkling; a tiny flag flutters. Slow low-angle camera rise. |
| 6 | ~5s | The tow truck drives in from the back-right; its hook arm swings toward CHIEF's scooter in the red zone. CHIEF stays frozen in his pose, oblivious. PIP slowly turns his head, eyes widening. Slight zoom toward the hook. |
| 7 | ~4s | The hook clamps CHIEF's own scooter; the stamp slams a "TOWED" mark; CHIEF is yanked off the podium and flails, face going from shocked to panicked; a comedic star-burst flashes. PIP beams. Quick punch-in then settle, small screen shake. |
| 8 | ~1s | CHIEF and his scooter get dragged off shrinking into the distance; PIP peels the boot off (it pops free) and gives a small friendly wave to camera. Camera returns to the exact Shot-1 framing. Hard cut at the end. |

## Assembly note
Put the 8 clips in order 1→8. Trim to ~32s total. Make sure the **last frame of Shot 8 matches the
first frame of Shot 1** so the video loops. Then add audio per [03-audio.md](03-audio.md).

## If your tool struggles
- Generate shorter (2–3s) motions and hold/extend the still if needed.
- Ask for "subtle animation, keep style" if the tool over-animates or changes the design.
- For Shot 7, if the action is too complex, split into two quick clips: (a) hook clamps scooter +
  "TOWED" slam, (b) CHIEF yanked away — then cut them together.
