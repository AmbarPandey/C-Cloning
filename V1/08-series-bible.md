# V1 — Series Bible (locked identity for all videos)

This keeps the series consistent so your characters build a recognizable identity over time. These
rules stay the **same in V1, V2, V3, …**. Only the *situation* and the *twist* change each video.

## The channel format (never changes)
- **Length:** ~25–40s vertical (9:16) animated Short.
- **Comedy:** clean, twist-ending, mute-first (understandable with sound off), advertiser-safe.
- **Every video ends with a real twist** — usually instant karma against the arrogant character.
- **Recurring cast + recurring look** = the identity viewers subscribe for.

## The recurring cast (locked designs — reuse the saved reference images)

### PIP — the lovable underdog (series lead / channel face)
- Small, round, kind, big eyes, **teal scarf**, paper-white body. ~2 heads tall.
- Always sympathetic; never the aggressor. The twist rewards PIP; PIP reacts with relief/joy.
- PIP is the character viewers fall in love with — feature PIP in thumbnails and the channel icon.

### CHIEF — the pompous authority (recurring foil / "villain you love to see lose")
- Rotund, chest-out, **teal warden jacket + peaked cap + yellow medal sash**, tiny mustache, smug.
- Always overconfident and power-abusing; his **own ego triggers his downfall** every episode.
- Recognizable "power object": his **giant stamp** (and often the **yellow boot**).

## Locked visual style
Flat-color 2D, thick black outlines, no gradients, minimal shading, chunky proportions, high contrast.
**Palette:** ink `#1A1A1A` · brand yellow `#FFD400` · paper `#FFF7E0` · sky `#BFE3F2` · asphalt
`#6E7076` · alert red `#E4322B` · teal `#2FB6A3`.

## Locked storytelling beats (every episode)
1. **Hook (0–2s):** show the conflict + hide a small clue (a "seed").
2. **Escalation:** the arrogant character abuses power / overreaches.
3. **Silence + anticipation:** drop the music right before the turn.
4. **Twist (~28s):** karma pays off the seed from the hook.
5. **Button + loop:** likeable close; last frame = first frame for a seamless replay.

## Locked audio rules
- Mute-first; music + SFX only. One deliberate silence beat right before the twist.
- No copyrighted music. Optional voices must reuse one fixed voice per character.

## Locked publishing style
- Title = curiosity + karma + one emoji + `#shorts`.
- Reuse the channel hashtag set. Feature PIP or the arrogant character's shocked face in thumbnails.

## What changes each video (the only variables)
- The **setting** (parking lot → beach, office, zoo, gym, …).
- The **arrogant character's scheme** and the **specific karma twist**.
- New **props/backgrounds** as needed — but the **cast designs never change**.
