## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF + GOAL DIAGRAM (0:00-0:02)

> **Cold-open inversion.** C1 does not establish anything. It shows the ending already in motion, then
> hard-cuts to the drawn rule of the world. No character enters frame. Nothing is explained.

**C1a — the cold payoff (0:00-0:015)**
- **VIS:** Close on the lock's scan panel, a gloved hand already smearing across it. The panel throws its **rejection mark** — `ALERT_RED` cross, hard flash — then again, then a third time, faster. Each refusal is louder. Cut on the third.
- **CAM:** Tight. Subject fills the frame. Motion already underway on frame one — **no push-in from a wide, no entrance.**
- **HOOK TEXT (edit layer, not baked into the render):** *"Locked out by his own face"*

**C1b — the goal diagram (0:015-0:02)**
- **VIS:** Hard cut to the gate: the lock's own pictogram strip on the panel — a `POP_TEAL` tick beside a clean face, an `ALERT_RED` cross beside a smeared one. Below it, a small manual keyhole on the lock's underside, and the celebration cake with thick `PAPER` icing on its table.
- **ACT:** PIP is at the gate in the same frame, quietly trying the manual keyhole with the little key — same goal, the boring method, already working.
- **CAM:** Hard cut. Static, medium-wide, held. This is the frame C8 returns to for the loop seam.
- **VO:** *"The lock only knows one face."*
- **SFX:** Three rejection buzzes, rising in pitch and speed; glove squeaking on glass; a small mechanical *clunk* from the keyhole; bed enters on the diagram cut.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked · PIP neutral

**Load-bearing flaw planted here:** the icing cake sits on CHIEF's side of the gate from frame 1, and the lock reads faces. Celebrating is what breaks it.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium shot of the gate. CHIEF bolts the smart lock onto it - a chunky `BRAND_YELLOW` device with a small screen. He leans in for a face scan; the screen flashes a **green checkmark**.
- **ACT:** CHIEF spins around and wags his gloved finger at PIP through the gate bars. Grins widely. PIP blinks, hands in scarf.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in ending on the green checkmark and CHIEF's smug face side by side.
- **VO:** *"Face. Recognized."*
- **SFX:** Power-up chime (lock activating); a digital *bleep-bleep* scan; green-check *ding*; a dismissive "heh".
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug - PIP patient.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** Close-medium on the lock's screen. CHIEF taps it repeatedly. Each tap adds a new icon: a fingerprint, a retinal scan, a longer passcode bar, a second face-scan icon. The lock glows brighter and more `BRAND_YELLOW` with each upgrade.
- **ACT:** CHIEF adds layer after layer of "security," each time turning to PIP with a bigger grin. PIP waits, unbothered, one hand in his scarf where the key is.
- **CAM:** Static with 3-frame holds on each new feature icon appearing.
- **VO:** *"More security. Obviously."*
- **SFX:** Digital *bloop* for each added feature (4 total, ascending pitch); lock glow hum intensifying; a smug chuckle.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating - PIP content.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** Wide shot. CHIEF demonstrates: he walks through the gate (green beep), turns, walks back through (green beep), does a little strut. The celebration cake is clearly visible on its table each time he passes it.
- **ACT:** CHIEF struts through twice, each pass more theatrical. He gestures as if showing off to an audience. PIP watches patiently from the other side.
- **CAM:** Static wide; slight push-in on the second pass framing CHIEF and the cake together.
- **VO:** *"In and out. Only him."*
- **SFX:** Two green *beep-boop* door opens; strut footsteps; a proud "hmph"; bed building to peak.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant - PIP patient.

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** CHIEF spots the cake. He grabs it with both gloves and takes an enormous, messy bite. **Thick white icing smears across his entire face** - nose, both cheeks, forehead, over one eye. He doesn't look in a mirror; he doesn't notice.
- **ACT:** He takes the bite on beat, then holds a triumphant pose with icing dripping off his chin, one fist raised.
- **CAM:** Medium shot, slight low angle (hero-angle). The icing on his face dominates.
- **VO:** *"Time to celebrate."* -> then silence.
- **SFX:** Bed swells to peak then **CUTS TO SILENCE**; a big squishy *chomp*; icing *splat* across face; only the ambient hum of the lock remains.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant (L3).

### C6 - PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** In near-silence, CHIEF struts to the gate to pass through again. He leans into the lock's scanner. The screen flashes **RED X**. He startles. Wipes his face slightly with one glove (smearing the icing further). Tries again - **RED X**. Wipes harder, frantic - **RED X**. He is locked out of his own gate by his own lock.
- **ACT:** Three scan attempts, each more desperate. After the third rejection, his shoulders slump. PIP raises one eyebrow.
- **CAM:** Static two-shot (CHIEF on one side, PIP on the other, gate between); 3-frame hold on each RED X.
- **VO:** (silence) optional whisper: *"...it didn't recognize him."*
- **SFX:** Silence except: scan *bleep*... **rejection buzz** (x3, each louder); the lock's idle hum; one frantic glove-wiping sound; PIP's single curious blink.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked - PIP curious.

### C7 - TWIST / FALSE VICTORY COLLAPSE (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** PIP calmly reaches into his `POP_TEAL` scarf, pulls out the **tiny brass key**, and inserts it into a small manual keyhole on the underside of the smart lock. A simple mechanical **click** and the gate swings open.
- **ACT:** PIP walks through calmly, hands behind his back. CHIEF's jaw drops - icing still covering his face, the smart lock's screen still showing RED X. PIP's face goes from calm to subtly gleeful.
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in on the key entering the lock, then settle wide; 0.5 s freeze on PIP walking through with CHIEF frozen in disbelief.
- **VO:** *"PIP had the backup key."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; a satisfying mechanical *click-clunk*; gate creak open; record-scratch as CHIEF's jaw drops; a single *drip* of icing hitting the ground.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked - PIP gleeful. <- the SHARE trigger.

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. PIP stands on the other side of the gate, politely holding it open. CHIEF, icing-faced and defeated, shuffles through the gate that PIP opened for him. PIP gives a small wave to camera.
- **ACT:** CHIEF hunches through, mortified. PIP waves. One last icing *drip* falls off CHIEF's nose.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out <=1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** A warm resolve chord; CHIEF's shuffling footstep; one last icing *drip*; button *ding*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF sheepish - PIP kind. **Loop seam:** C8 reuses C1's exact framing, background plate and gate position - only CHIEF's icing-covered face and the open gate differ, so the replay re-reads the key handoff and the cake.
