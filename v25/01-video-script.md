> **Premise:** a domino contest — first chain to topple the marker at the finish line wins. PIP lays a short
> honest run. CHIEF builds an enormous elaborate chain and, to make it grander, **routes it across PIP's
> line**. His chain fires beautifully, reaches the finish first, and topples the marker — he wins. Then it
> keeps going, because a chain doesn't stop: it crosses at the point he built, sets **PIP's** run off, and
> PIP's chain topples the prize onto PIP's side. His own chain won it for the other guy.
> **Seeds:** **(A)** the finish line + marker + chain-reaches-line pictogram, **(B)** the **crossing point**
> he builds at 0:04 where his run passes over PIP's.

## Scene-by-scene

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF (0:00–0:01.5)
- **VIS:** ECU on a `PAPER` tile **already toppling sideways**, mid-fall, its edge about to strike a `POP_TEAL` tile standing at right angles to it. Contact has not happened yet. No table, no faces, no context.
- **CAM:** Tight ECU, static, 45 frames. Motion underway on frame 1.
- **VO:** *"Three, two—"*
- **SFX:** No music. One `SFX_tile_topple_v1`, suspended.

### C1b — GOAL DIAGRAM (0:01.5–0:02)
- **VIS:** Flat schematic: a bold `ALERT_RED` **finish line** with a `BRAND_YELLOW` **prize marker** standing on it, and beside it a pictogram — a short row of tiles, an arrow, the line, a `POP_TEAL` **tick**. Wordless.
- **CAM:** Flat MED, dead centre, held 15 frames.
- **SFX:** Music **enters**. `SFX_ting_v1`.

### C2 — REWIND / SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** A plain table. The `ALERT_RED` finish line runs across it with the `BRAND_YELLOW` marker standing on it. PIP lays `POP_TEAL` tiles in a short straight run toward the line. CHIEF lays `PAPER` tiles in a grand looping arc.
- **ACT:** To make his run more impressive, CHIEF routes it in a wide sweep that **passes directly over PIP's line**, laying two tiles at the crossing point (**SEED B**, casual, unremarked). PIP glances at the crossing, then carries on.
- **CAM:** MED two-shot, slow push-in ending on the crossing point.
- **VO:** *"One of them built four tiles."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_tile_set_v1` ×4 (PIP, even) · `SFX_tile_set_v1` ×6 (CHIEF, fast) · `SFX_button_v1` "pfft"
- **EMO:** CHIEF `gloating` / PIP `neutral`

- **ACT:** He looks at PIP's four tiles and laughs.
