**Story:** a domino contest — first chain to topple the marker wins. PIP lays four tiles. CHIEF builds a
monument and, to make it grander, routes it **across PIP's line**. His chain fires, reaches the finish
first, topples the marker — he wins. Then it keeps going, because a chain doesn't stop: it hits the crossing
he built, sets **PIP's** run off, and PIP's four tiles topple the prize onto PIP's side.

**Seeds:** the finish line + marker + pictogram, and the **crossing point** he builds at 0:04.

## What's new
- **First `CM-E2` Chain Reaction**, and `NP1×SC3` has never been paired. `SC3` returns for the first time since v3.
- **The clearest scoreboard in the batch.** Two runs, side by side, aimed at one line, in frame together for fourteen seconds. The retention analysis identified v3's side-by-side contest as its single most transferable asset; this is that structure at its most literal.
- **The false floor lands inside the silence.** The marker falls **for CHIEF at 0:19**, in the quiet — so the audience hears him win, and then hears the clatter carry on afterwards. That gap between *"he won"* and *"why is it still going"* is the entire eleven seconds.
- **Two tokens, two pitches.** CHIEF's tiles are `PAPER`, PIP's are `POP_TEAL` — and the run SFX has two variants a musical third apart. At 0:29 the chain **audibly changes pitch** at the crossing: his run becomes PIP's. The twist is delivered to the ear with no words at all.
- **The same asset, twice, opposite outcome.** `SFX_marker_fall_v1` plays at 0:19 for CHIEF and at 0:30 for PIP. Same recording, same level relationship — the repetition *is* the irony.

## A deliberate separation I want to flag
v18 uses `TW10 Chain-Reaction Payoff` as its **twist container**. v25 uses `CM-E2 Chain Reaction` as its
**comedy mechanic** with `TW2 Irony Reversal` as the container. Pairing `CM-E2` **with** `TW10` would be
precisely the core-collapse defect the base audit found on v7 and four of the v8–v17 batch — one core
wearing two hats. So the batch's two chain-driven episodes are built on opposite axes on purpose, and
neither declares the same core twice.

## Compliance notes
- Comedy core ≠ twist core (Chain Reaction ≠ Irony Reversal) ✓ · no invented IDs ✓ · score inputs shown ✓ · `NP1×SC3` fresh ✓.
- Distinct from **v3**, the other `SC3` episode: v3 was vertical (a tower collapsing), v25 is horizontal and networked (a chain re-routing), with a different pattern, mechanic and twist.
- **The chain must be traceable end to end** — no camera jumps across the run. On rewatch a viewer will check that the path was genuinely there from C2, and it has to survive that check. This is the episode's primary replay driver.
- Colour tokens only — CHIEF tiles `PAPER`, PIP tiles `POP_TEAL`, line `ALERT_RED`, marker `BRAND_YELLOW`, table `ASPHALT`.
- CHIEF keeps cap + sash + medals throughout.
- Ad-safety: light tiles on a table, nothing falls on anyone, no breakage.
