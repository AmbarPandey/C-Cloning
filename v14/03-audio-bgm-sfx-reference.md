## 3. The silence beat (0:16-0:27) - the single most important audio move

An **11-second musical silence**, cut **mid-phrase** on the frame the tide water enters CHIEF's channel.

**Only these sounds are permitted inside it:**

| Time | Sound | Level | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:16-0:27 | `SFX_ocean_ambient_v1` (continuous, now prominent) | **-14 dB** | The ocean is the only constant -- it is both the ambient bed and the antagonist. Its steady presence underscores that this was always inevitable |
| 0:16 | `SFX_wave_rush_v1` | **-6 dB** | The wave that enters the channel. Exposed fully by the music cut -- loudest discrete sound in the silence |
| 0:16.5 | `SFX_water_flow_v1` (begins, continuous and building) | -10 dB, rising to -8 dB by 0:22 | Water flowing through channel -- the sound of CHIEF's own engineering working against him |
| 0:18 | `SFX_sand_crumble_big_v1` (small, preview) | -16 dB | CHIEF's scramble down -- feet crumble the turret slightly. A preview of what's coming |
| 0:20 | `SFX_water_gurgle_v1` | -12 dB | Water reaching the moat and beginning to pool -- bubbling, filling |
| 0:22-0:23.5 | `SFX_sand_dissolve_v1` (x2) | -12 dB | Sand dumped into channel dissolves twice -- the helpless sound of material failing |
| 0:23.5 | `SFX_body_sit_sand_v1` | -10 dB | CHIEF sitting on channel entrance -- heavy, desperate |
| 0:24 | `SFX_water_flow_v1` (volume spike) | **-6 dB** | Water diverts and accelerates through third channel -- louder, faster, the system is winning |
| 0:25 | `SFX_cap_grab_v1` | -14 dB | Cap yanked off head |
| 0:25.5 | `SFX_water_splash_small_v1` (x3, rapid) | -12 dB | Cap scooping water -- futile splashes |
| 0:26 | `SFX_structural_crack_v1` | **-8 dB** | The tower wall cracking. The sound of imminent collapse |
| 0:26.5 | (ocean ambient only -- 0.5 s of just the waves) | -- | The moment of total inevitability before the collapse |

- **No VO** in this window except an optional whispered *"...no."* at ~0:22, <=0.5 s.
- **No music, no stings.** The water flow and dissolving sand must own the space.

> **The best sound in the silence is the 0.5 s of nothing at 0:26.5.** After the scooping fails and the
> crack appears, just the steady ocean ambience remains. The audience knows the collapse is coming.
> That 0.5 s of inevitability is more powerful than any stinger.

## 4. Mix notes

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Overall loudness target | -14 LUFS (YouTube Short standard) |
| BGM ducking under VO | -6 dB duck, 100 ms attack, 400 ms release |
| SFX priority during silence | water flow > structural sounds > body/action > ambient |
| The wave_rush at 0:16 | must be the loudest single SFX hit in the entire video (exposed by music absence) |
| Ocean ambient behavior | constant throughout but level varies: -22 dB (background) to -14 dB (during silence) |
| VO style | dry, close-mic, deadpan, short phrases only. Never more than 6 words. American male neutral |
| Stereo width | SFX: 60% width. BGM: 80% width. VO: center mono. AMB: 100% width |
