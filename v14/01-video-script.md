> access. He digs a deep moat around his fortress for protection. PIP, unbothered, sits cross-legged
> behind the wall and starts building a tiny sand turtle (SEED A). CHIEF's fortress grows taller and
> wider, his moat deeper -- but the moat channels create a direct funnel for the incoming tide (SEED B).

> collapsing it inward on him. Meanwhile PIP's tiny turtle, sitting on higher ground, remains intact.

> and **(B) the moat channels** -- CHIEF digs them in C3 and the audience can see they slope toward the sea.

## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend - **VIS** visual/staging - **ACT** action - **CAM** camera - **VO** narration (optional layer) - **SFX** sound - **EMO** emotion

### C1 - HOOK + SEED (0:00-0:02)
- **VIS:** A bright `SKY`-blue sky over a `PAPER`-coloured sand beach. Ocean (`SKY` blue-green) visible in the background with gentle wave foam. PIP kneels on a small raised mound (6 inches higher than surrounding beach), shaping a tiny lump of sand (SEED A: the future turtle). CHIEF stomps into frame from the left, kicks a spray of sand at PIP (PIP flinches, grains on his scarf), and jams a small `BRAND_YELLOW` flag into a half-built sand wall between PIP and the ocean.
- **ACT:** CHIEF kicks sand, plants flag aggressively. PIP flinches but stays on his mound.
- **CAM:** Static wide establishing (this exact framing returns in C8).
- **VO:** *"One beach. One wall."*
- **SFX:** Ocean ambience; a sandy *kick-scatter*; flag *jab* into sand; upbeat comedy bed enters.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug - PIP startled.

### C2 - SETUP (0:02-0:06)
- **VIS:** CHIEF grabs a small flat shovel (was stuck in the sand beside him) and starts building the wall higher, scooping sand rapidly. He begins digging a **trench** on the ocean-facing side of the wall (SEED B: the moat). The trench slopes slightly downhill toward the waterline. PIP behind the wall: wipes sand grains off his `POP_TEAL` scarf, blinks once, then returns to his mound and continues shaping his tiny sand lump with careful pats. A gentle wave laps the beach 10 feet behind CHIEF's construction.
- **ACT:** CHIEF digs and builds. PIP calmly shapes his turtle.
- **CAM:** Medium-wide, static. Both characters visible with the wall between them.
- **VO:** *"Higher. Deeper. That'll show him."*
- **SFX:** Shovel *scrape-scrape*; sand *plop*; ocean lapping gently; the comedy bed builds with a ukulele layer.
- **EMO:** CHIEF determined - PIP calm.

### C3 - ESCALATION 1 (0:06-0:11)
- **VIS:** The wall is now at CHIEF's shoulder height. CHIEF is digging the moat deeper, creating three distinct **channel arms** that extend outward toward the waterline like fingers. He packs the channel walls smooth (they are well-engineered, even slopes). He adds decorative turrets to the fortress top, each with a tiny `BRAND_YELLOW` flag. The waterline has crept ~2 feet closer (visible if you watch the foam line). PIP is not in frame.
- **ACT:** CHIEF sculpts channels, adds turrets, admires his work from different angles.
- **CAM:** Static medium, focused on CHIEF and his fortress. The channel slopes toward the waterline are visible in the bottom of frame.
- **VO:** *"A moat. For protection."*
- **SFX:** Sand *pat-pat-pat*; a wet *squelch* as the moat gets deeper; medal *jingle* as CHIEF leans over; the comedy bed adds tropical percussion.
- **EMO:** CHIEF proud.

### C4 - ESCALATION 2 (0:11-0:16)
- **VIS:** The fortress is now massive -- multiple towers, crenellated battlements, a deep moat ring with three channel arms extending 4-5 feet seaward. CHIEF climbs ON TOP of the tallest tower (it flexes slightly under his weight). He stands with arms spread wide, cape-pose, medals glinting. **The waterline is now visibly close** -- the nearest foam touch is inches from the outermost channel tip. A thin glisten of water connects to the channel end. CHIEF, looking inland (at PIP's direction, gloating), does not see this.
- **ACT:** CHIEF poses triumphantly on his fortress. Water creeps to channel tips unnoticed.
- **CAM:** Slow push-in (100% to 106%) ending on a split composition: CHIEF posing above, the water-meets-channel contact below.
- **VO:** *"The biggest castle on the beach. And the deepest moat."*
- **SFX:** Ocean *surge* slightly louder; sand *creak* under CHIEF's weight on the tower; the bed reaches its densest/brightest point with a steel drum hit.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant (oblivious).

### C5 - ANTICIPATION (0:16-0:22)
- **VIS:** A medium wave rolls in. Its leading edge hits the channel entrance. Water flows into the first channel smoothly -- like it was designed for this. The water accelerates as the channel slopes downhill toward the moat. CHIEF, still on his tower, looks down. His triumphant pose freezes. His expression crashes (4-frame transition from triumph to horror). He scrambles down the tower side (sand crumbles under his feet) and tries to block the channel entrance with his gloved hands. Water flows between his fingers. **Music cuts to silence.**
- **ACT:** CHIEF's triumph becomes panic as the water enters his own channels.
- **CAM:** Medium shot, slightly low angle. The water flowing through the channel and CHIEF's panicked face share the frame.
- **VO:** *"Wait--"* (cuts off with the music)
- **SFX:** A rushing *gurgle* as water enters the channel; sand *crumbling*; a wet *splash* as CHIEF lands at the base; **music cuts to silence**; only ocean ambience and flowing water remain.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked (L3).

### C6 - ANTICIPATION / PATTERN BREAK (0:22-0:27)
- **VIS:** Near-silence except water sounds. All three channels are now flowing simultaneously. Three panic attempts:
  - **Attempt 1 (0:22-0:23.5):** CHIEF scoops armfuls of dry sand and dumps them in a channel entrance. The water dissolves the sand instantly and keeps flowing.
  - **Attempt 2 (0:23.5-0:25):** CHIEF sits bodily on the second channel entrance. Water diverts and accelerates through the third channel. The moat is filling.
  - **Attempt 3 (0:25-0:27):** CHIEF yanks off his cap and tries to scoop water OUT of the moat. But the walls are saturated -- they lean inward, softening. Cracks appear in the main tower.
- **ACT:** Each attempt fails worse than the last. The fortress is doomed.
- **CAM:** Static medium. The water level in the moat rises visibly across the 5 seconds.
- **SFX:** Water *rushing* and *gurgling* through channels; wet sand *dissolving*; cap *scooping* water; a low structural *creak* from the walls.
- **EMO:** CHIEF frantic.

### C7 - TWIST (0:27-0:31)
- **VIS:** The main tower leans, cracks, and the entire fortress **collapses inward** with a wet *crumble*. Sand piles around CHIEF, burying him to the waist. His `BRAND_YELLOW` flags topple and lie flat in the wet sand. His shovel sticks up from the debris at an angle. Camera pulls back to reveal the full beach: on the raised mound (3 feet away, untouched by water), PIP's tiny sand **turtle** is perfectly intact -- detailed, with a cute face, four flippers, and shell pattern. PIP sits cross-legged beside it, patting one last grain into place. He looks over at CHIEF and tilts his head quizzically.
- **ACT:** Fortress collapses on CHIEF. Reveal of PIP's untouched turtle.
- **CAM:** Medium on CHIEF for the collapse, then a steady pull-back to wide revealing PIP and the turtle on the dry mound. 0.5 s hold on the contrast.
- **VO:** *"Huh. Still standing."*
- **SFX:** Music **SLAMS back** at 0:27; a wet *crumble-collapse*; sand *settling*; a warm resolve melody enters.
- **EMO:** CHIEF shocked - PIP content.

### C8 - PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31-0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. The wide beach framing: CHIEF half-buried in wet collapsed sand (where his fortress was), flags scattered flat, cap soggy. PIP on his raised mound with the perfectly intact turtle beside him. PIP extends a hand toward CHIEF (offering to help pull him out). PIP turns to camera and gives a **small wave** (3-frame wave). The tide gently recedes behind them.
- **ACT:** PIP offers help, waves. CHIEF slumps.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 wide framing. Hard cut out.
- **VO:** *"Every time."*
- **SFX:** Warm resolve melody; a final wave *recede* sound; PIP's wave *whoosh*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF defeated - PIP kind.
