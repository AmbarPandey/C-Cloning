# Library 2 — Comedy Mechanics Library

## Purpose
Discover **why people laugh**, reduced to reusable *mechanics* (cognitive processes) rather than genres, styles, or topics.

## First principle
> Laughter = **a violated prediction that feels safe.** Two required conditions: **incongruity** (reality ≠ prediction) + **benign framing** (the violation isn't threatening). Advertiser-safe humor literally exploits the benign condition.

## Knowledge stored

### The definitive taxonomy — 15 mechanics in 5 families
```
FAMILY A — EXPECTATION ENGINE (the cause):
   A1 Expectation Violation · A2 Misdirection · A3 Delayed Realization · A4 Visual Surprise
FAMILY B — LOGIC BENDERS:  B1 Irony · B2 Literal Interpretation · B3 Absurd Logic
FAMILY C — STATUS SHIFTS:  C1 Role Reversal · C2 Overconfidence Collapse · C3 Instant Karma · C4 False Victory
FAMILY D — REACTION AMPLIFIERS (never standalone): D1 Overreaction · D2 Underreaction · D3 Deadpan Contrast · D4 Embarrassment
FAMILY E — STRUCTURAL DRIVERS: E1 Escalation · E2 Chain Reaction
```
- **Family A** delivers the laugh · **B/C** supply the incongruity · **D** amplifies · **E** paces.

### Universal comedy anatomy
`Setup → Prediction → Violation → Recognition → Safety Check → Relief → Laughter`

## Important decisions
- Emphasize A1 + A2 (the writing moat) and C2/C3/B1 (highest-satisfaction, ad-safe payoffs).
- Almost never use B2 Literal as a *primary* (predictability ceiling) or Coincidence (cheap).
- Family-D mechanics are **amplifiers only** — never the joke itself.
