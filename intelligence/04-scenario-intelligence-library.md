# Library 4 — Scenario Intelligence Library

## Purpose
Discover **why certain situations repeatedly generate viral animated Shorts** — as reusable frameworks, not locations or story ideas.

## Value law
> The best scenarios **pre-install the setup for free**: expectation + roles + conflict arrive before a word is written. If a scenario needs explaining, it's weak.

## Knowledge stored

### 10 scenario archetypes in 5 families
```
FAMILY I  — POWER & STATUS:   SC1 Authority · SC2 Service Exchange
FAMILY II — STAKES & WINNING: SC3 Competition
FAMILY III— RELATIONSHIPS:    SC4 Family/Domestic · SC5 Romance/Dating
FAMILY IV — DANGER & JUSTICE: SC6 Crime & Justice · SC7 Danger/Survival
FAMILY V  — MODERN & UNIVERSAL: SC8 Everyday Friction · SC9 Technology/Social ⚠️ · SC10 Animals-as-People
```

### Scenario anatomy
`Environment → Roles (status gap) → Goals → Conflict → Expectation → Pressure → Twist Opportunity`

The **status gap** is the master component: bigger, clearer power difference = bigger reversal payoff.

## The Company Standard (locked) — "The Universal Status-Gap Framework"
A scenario is approved only if it has all five: instant recognition (0s explanation), a clear status gap/stakes, a built-in expectation to violate, global understanding (works muted), and evergreen + advertiser-safe.

### Priority tiers
- **Daily:** SC1 Authority · SC3 Competition · SC10 Animals.
- **Support:** SC2 Service · SC8 Everyday.
- **Variety:** SC4 Family · SC5 Romance · SC6 Crime (bloodless) · SC7 Danger (cartoony).
- **Rare:** SC9 Tech — the only non-evergreen family (Evergreen capped at 3).

## Relationships
- Fuels the Twist ([L3](03-narrative-twist-library.md)) with its status gap.
- Hosts the Comedy Mechanic ([L2](02-comedy-mechanics-library.md)) and the Narrative Pattern ([L6](06-narrative-pattern-library.md)).

## How later stages consume it
The Content Matrix selects one `SC` node, validated for pattern↔scenario compatibility and the AdSafe gate.

## Examples
Idea A1 uses **SC1 Authority** (enforcement officer vs. tiny citizen) — a maximal, instantly recognizable status gap.

## Important decisions
- ~80% of scenario failures = a flat status gap or non-global reference.
- Heavy-reframe patterns (NP4/NP7) are forbidden on low-stakes scenarios (SC8/SC9).
- SC9 (Tech) is quota-limited due to its evergreen penalty.
