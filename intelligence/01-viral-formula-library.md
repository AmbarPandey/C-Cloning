# Library 1 — Viral Formula Library

## Purpose
Discover the reusable **structure** behind viral animated Shorts — the topic-agnostic skeleton that reliably produces a predictable emotional reaction.

## Founding principle
> Virality is caused by the **structure a idea is delivered through**, not the idea itself. A weak idea in a strong structure beats a strong idea with no structure.

### The Master Formula (locked) — "The Plot-Twist Formula"
```
Recognizable Hook → False Comfort → Visual Escalation → Pattern Break → Twist → Hard Cut Ending
```
Wrapped by three constants: runs through a **recurring cast** (loyalty), reads with **sound off** (global), and is **advertiser-safe** (RPM).

| Beat | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Recognizable Hook | Wins the first 3s via borrowed familiarity |
| False Comfort | Confirms the prediction → sets the trap |
| Visual Escalation | Builds momentum so viewers don't swipe |
| Pattern Break | Re-hooks at the drop-off zone |
| Twist | The prediction-error payload (share + replay) |
| Hard Cut | Ends on the peak → high avg-view% + replays |

## Examples

## Important decisions
- **Winner = Expectation Subversion.** The other top formulas are not discarded — they *stack into* it (Escalation as ramp, Language-Free as delivery, Character Loyalty as amplifier).
- The formula is **fixed and universal**; variety comes from patterns/scenarios/twists, not from changing the skeleton.
- 90% of failures trace to a **weak twist** or a **slow hook** — diagnose those first.
