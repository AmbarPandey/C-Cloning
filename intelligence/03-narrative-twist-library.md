# Library 3 — Narrative Twist Intelligence Library

## Purpose
Reverse-engineer **what makes a twist unforgettable and repeatably successful** — treating the twist as an engineering component.

## Defining law
> **Unpredictable in foresight, inevitable in hindsight.** Guessable = dead; random = cheap. The craft lives in the narrow band between the two, and that band is controlled by the **seed**.

## Why the twist is the highest-leverage moment
It manufactures all four growth currencies at once: **Replay** (rewatch for the seed), **Share** ("you must see this"), **Comment** ("didn't see it coming"), and **Memory** (recontextualization).

### Twist anatomy
`Prediction → Confidence → SEED → Violation → Recognition → Recontextualization → Satisfaction → Replay Trigger`

The **seed** is the master variable: no seed → random; loud seed → predictable; subtle seed → magic.

## The Company Standard (locked) — "The Seeded Reversal Framework"
1. Raise a confident wrong prediction · 2. Plant a hidden seed · 3. Deliver one core reversal · 4. Keep it fair + bloodless · 5. Hard cut on the reveal · 6. Reward the rewatch. Constraints: one core, reads muted, runs through the recurring cast.
