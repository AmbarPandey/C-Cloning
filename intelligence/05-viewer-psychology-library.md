# Library 5 — Viewer Psychology Intelligence Library

## Purpose
Build a **Viewer Behavior Operating System** — the predictable rules for *why viewers act* while watching Shorts, always tied back to the other libraries.

## Founding law
> **Emotion is the cause; behavior is the product; the algorithm pays for the product.** Design for an *action*, never a feeling.

## Knowledge stored

### Actions (measured) vs Drivers (internal causes)
```
ACTIONS:  B1 Watch-to-End · B2 Replay · B3 Swipe-Away (enemy) · B4 Like ·
          B5 Comment · B6 Share · B7 Subscribe · B8 Return
DRIVERS:  Curiosity · Anticipation · Surprise · Satisfaction ·
          Justice · Recognition · Social Validation · Trust/Parasocial
```

### Behavior value ranking (Shorts monetization)
`Anti-Swipe > Completion > Replay > Share > Subscribe > Comment > Return > Like`

### Behavior journey
`First Frame → Hook → Engagement → Prediction → Comedy → Twist → Replay? → Share? → Subscribe?`
Highest-leakage points: **First Frame→Hook** (swipe) and **Twist→Replay** (no seed).

## The Company Standard (locked) — "The Predict-Then-Reward Loop"
1. Hook curiosity (beat swipe) · 2. Build anticipation (watch-to-end) · 3. Force a prediction · 4. Violate + satisfy (share) · 5. Plant a seed (replay) · 6. Recurring-cast payoff (subscribe). Every video moves through all six; a missing step is a leaked behavior.

### Driver → Action map
Curiosity→watch · Anticipation→finish · Surprise→share+replay · Justice→comment · Trust→subscribe.

## Relationships
- Sets the *target action* that a Narrative Pattern ([L6](06-narrative-pattern-library.md)) is chosen to drive.
- Maps Business Goals to behaviors inside [Library 7](07-virality-intelligence-database.md).

## How later stages consume it
The Content Matrix resolves a Business Goal into a target Behavior (Layer 1 of the funnel). The Script Compiler audits retention/replay/share/subscribe drivers.

## Examples
Idea A1 targets **Share**, driven by Surprise + Justice + Social Validation from the karma twist.

## Important decisions
- Fix-it 80/20: swipe → fix the hook; no replay → add the seed; no share → sharpen the twist; no subs → feature the cast.
- Never optimize for Likes (weakest signal).
