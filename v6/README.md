**Story in one line:** a sweet stall posts its rate as a wordless pictogram — one sweet, one coin. CHIEF
slams down a huge flashy purse, shovels a mountain of sweets while gloating at tiny PIP, then slams it on
the balance scale to be weighed against his money. The purse holds exactly **one coin.** He removes handful
after handful until the pans balance: one sweet. The mountain goes to the dog.

**The seeds:** the **rate pictogram** (1 sweet ↔ 1 coin) on the stall in frame 1, and the **purse falling
open for three frames at 0:04**, showing it nearly empty.

## What's new in v6

**Fresh scenario.** `SC2 Service Exchange` is used for the first time, making **NP3×SC2 a fresh
pattern×scenario pair**. `TW2 Irony Reversal` recurs from v4, but by a completely different engine: v4 was
**irony of geometry** (he walled himself out); v6 is **irony of arithmetic** (his greed prices him out).

**The strongest retention device in the series so far — and it needs no sound or text.** Once the rate
pictogram is established in frame 1, **the balance scale becomes an on-screen progress bar.** Every scoop
visibly raises the price, so the viewer has a continuous, wordless reason to keep watching: they want to
see the reckoning. Shorts are watched muted by default, which makes a purely visual meter far more valuable
than a narrated hook.

**One colour decision carries the whole thesis.** The sweets and the coin are both `BRAND_YELLOW` — the
object of his greed and the thing that limits it are literally the same colour. `ALERT_RED` is reserved for
the purse, so red is the thing that betrays him. The scale stays neutral grey: an impartial machine.

**The VO is one sentence split across the silence.** *"He got it."* at 0:16 → 11 seconds of quiet → *"Then
he paid for it."* at 0:27. The turn on the word "paid" is the entire punchline.

**Signature-sound inversion.** v1 = stamp · v2 = booster · v3 = block clack · v4 = panel slam ·
v5 = discovery sting. **v6 = the sweet clatter**, escalating through four sizes (S→M→L→XL) as his greed
grows, then inverted by *scale*: answered by a single delicate coin `tink` and resolved by one clean
balance `ting`. Ten seconds of loud abundance, defeated by one small precise note.

## Content-safety note I want to flag
The premise involves someone being unable to afford something, so I've deliberately built it so the target
is **vanity, not hardship**: CHIEF's purse is huge, flashy and ostentatious — he is a show-off whose purse
turns out to be empty — and he is never denied anything he *needs*, only something he was greedily hoarding.
That guardrail is written into the compliance checklists in files 01, 02 and 04. Worth keeping in mind if
you ever revisit this premise.
