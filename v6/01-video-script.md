## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend — **VIS** visual/staging · **ACT** action · **CAM** camera · **VO** narration (optional layer) · **SFX** sound · **EMO** emotion

### C1 — HOOK + SEED (0:00–0:02)
- **VIS:** A fairground sweet stall. `POP_TEAL`-striped awning, `PAPER` counter, glass jars of `BRAND_YELLOW` sweets. Centre of the counter: a **two-pan brass balance scale** (`ASPHALT` metal). Propped on the stall front: the **rate pictogram** — a single sweet shape, an `INK` equals sign, a single coin shape (**SEED A** — no words, universally readable). Tiny PIP stands behind the counter. A row of cups, smallest to largest.
- **ACT:** CHIEF struts up and **slams a huge, ostentatious `ALERT_RED` coin purse** onto the counter, then seizes the **largest** cup off the stack.
- **CAM:** Static wide establishing (this exact framing returns in C8).
- **VO:** *"The rate was posted. One sweet, one coin."*
- **SFX:** Fairground bed (low); a "boing" per strut step; a heavy purse *thud*; a cup *clack*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug · PIP worried.

### C2 — SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** Medium two-shot across the counter.
- **ACT:** CHIEF starts scooping sweets into the cup. PIP raises a small hand and **politely points at the rate pictogram**. CHIEF waves him off without looking, gloating. **As his elbow knocks it, the huge purse tips open for a moment — and it is almost empty** (**SEED B**).
- **CAM:** Slow push-in that ends framed on the purse as it falls open.
- **VO:** *"He didn't read it."*
- **SFX:** Scoop *clatter* ×2; a dismissive "pfft"; a soft *coin rattle* — thin and sparse, the sound of not-very-much.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating · PIP worried.

### C3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:06–0:11)
- **VIS:** The cup filling — sweets mounding above the rim.
- **ACT:** Scoop, scoop, scoop, each one more careless than the last. PIP glances between the growing pile and the scale, eyes widening.
- **CAM:** Static; a 3-frame impact hold on every second scoop.
- **VO:** *"He didn't need to. He was rich."*
- **SFX:** Rhythmic scoop *clatters*, rising in pitch; sweets *pattering* onto the pile.
- **EMO:** CHIEF gloating · PIP worried.

### C4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:11–0:16)
- **VIS:** He has abandoned the scoop and is shovelling with **both hands** — an absurd `BRAND_YELLOW` mountain overflowing the cup, sweets tumbling onto the counter.
- **ACT:** He gloats at PIP over the top of the pile, chest out, chin high.
- **CAM:** Slight push-in; 3-frame hold as the last handful lands.
- **VO:** *"Or so the purse suggested."*
- **SFX:** Fast double-handed *clatter* combo; sweets spilling; a proud fanfare stab.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant · PIP alarmed.
- **Note:** this is the overreach — the more he takes, the worse his position becomes. Every scoop is a step *away* from what he wants.

### C5 — ANTICIPATION (0:16–0:22)
- **VIS:** He **SLAMS** the overflowing cup onto the scale's left pan and plants his other hand out, palm up, waiting to be told the total — chest out, utterly certain. The pans begin to move.
- **ACT:** Long proud held tableau. As far as he is concerned, he has won.
- **CAM:** Low hero-angle push-in that settles into the hold, the mountain towering in frame.
- **VO:** *"He got it."* → then silence.
- **SFX:** Bed swells to a peak, then **CUTS TO SILENCE**; only the slow metallic *groan* of the scale settling.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant (L3).

### C6 — PATTERN BREAK (0:22–0:27)
- **VIS:** In near-silence, PIP calmly picks up the huge `ALERT_RED` purse, turns it over above the right pan — and **exactly one coin drops out.** It lands with a small, lonely *tink*. The sweets pan **crashes to the bottom**; the coin pan flies up.
- **ACT:** CHIEF's grin dies in three stages. He looks at the purse, shakes it, turns it inside out. Nothing. One sweat bead.
- **CAM:** Static two-shot on the scale, then a slow tilt to CHIEF's face as it drains.
- **VO:** (silence — let it breathe) optional whisper *"…one."*
- **SFX:** Silence, broken only by the single coin *tink*, the pan *crash*, and a small empty purse *flap*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF dawning horror · PIP patient.

### C7 — TWIST / IRONY REVERSAL (0:27–0:31)
- **VIS:** CHIEF frantically starts putting sweets **back** — handful after handful, faster and faster, the two pans creeping toward each other. The mountain shrinks. And shrinks. Until the pans hang **perfectly level**, with **one single sweet** in the left pan against one coin in the right.
- **ACT:** His face runs the **3-stage snap** triumphant → shocked → panicked. PIP watches, then beams.
- **CAM:** Quick punch-in on the balancing pans; ~0.5 s freeze on the level scale — one sweet, one coin.
- **VO:** *"Then he paid for it."*
- **SFX:** Music SLAMS back; a rapid reverse-clatter of returning sweets; the pans settling with a delicate *ting*; record-scratch.
- **EMO:** CHIEF panicked · PIP gleeful. **← the SHARE trigger.**

### C8 — PAYOFF / LOOP (0:31–0:32)
- **VIS:** Composition matches C1. CHIEF stands holding **one tiny sweet** between two oversized gloves, mortified, the empty `ALERT_RED` purse flat on the counter. Beside him, PIP tips the whole returned mountain into a big cup and sets it down for **BUD**, who is delighted.
- **ACT:** PIP turns and gives a small wave to camera. BUD's tail wags. CHIEF stares at his single sweet.
- **CAM:** Return to C1 framing; hard cut out ≤1 s after the wave.
- **VO:** *"Every time."* (series button; also seeds the loop)
- **SFX:** A cascade of sweets into BUD's cup; a happy *munch*; warm resolve chord; button *ding*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF sheepish · PIP relieved. **Loop seam:** C8 reuses C1's exact framing, background plate and horizon — only the scale state, the purse and the cast's holdings differ, so the replay re-reads the rate sign and the purse with the ending in mind.
