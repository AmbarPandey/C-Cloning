## ⚠️ Format decision you should know about
Any **vertical or square** video up to **3 minutes** is automatically categorised as a **Short** by
YouTube ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)). A 1:00–2:00 vertical video
would therefore land in the Shorts feed, not as long form. **v5 is specced 16:9 horizontal** so it is
genuinely treated as long form. Everything downstream (framing, thumbnail, composition) assumes 16:9.

## Why v5 is a labelled Mode B experiment (and why that's correct)

Stage 4 requires roughly **one exploration video per five deterministic ones**, each stating a hypothesis.
v5 is that slot — and long form is itself an untested format for this channel, so the honest label fits.

```
ViralityPotential = 0.30·Share + 0.25·Replay + 0.20·Evergreen + 0.15·AdSafe + 0.10·Ease
Share 8 · Replay 10 (TM3 seed present ⇒ ×1.0) · Evergreen 10 · AdSafe 8 (SC6 needs bloodless care) · Ease 4
                  = 2.40 + 2.50 + 2.00 + 1.20 + 0.40 = 8.50
CompatibilityChain: NP4↔SC6 5/5 · SC6↔CM-A2 5/5 · CM-A2↔TW5 5/5 · TW5↔FM 5/5 = 1.00
Confidence (Medium — NP4/TW5 are the unproven "premium/replay" tier) = 0.7
DifficultyPenalty = 0.0833 × 5 = 0.42   (Difficulty 5 — longest runtime, new environment, 2 new characters)
FinalScore = 8.50 × 1.00 × 0.7 − 0.42 = 5.53 ⇒ 5.5
Hard gate: AdSafe 8 ≥ 4 · one pattern · one twist core · TM3 seed present (required for Replay)
           · NP4+SC6 is not a forbidden pair (NP4/NP7 + SC8/SC9 would be) ⇒ valid.
```

**Hypothesis being tested:** *the premium/replay tier (NP4 Hidden Truth + TW5 Hidden Cause) under-performs
in 32 seconds because a hidden-cause mystery needs room to plant and misdirect — but at 90 seconds it
should outperform the daily workhorses on **replay rate and average view duration**, even at a lower
predicted share rate.*
**Success metric:** replay rate and AVD% beat the v1–v4 average. If they don't, the premium tier stays
off the daily queue and long form reverts to workhorse twists (TW2/TW3/TW4).

## Scene-by-scene script

> Legend — **VIS** visual/staging · **ACT** action · **CAM** camera · **VO** narration (optional layer) · **SFX** sound · **EMO** emotion · **RET** retention function

### ACT 1 — THE CRIME (0:00–0:12)

#### S1 — COLD-OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:03)
- **VIS:** Extreme close-up, 16:9, on an **empty pie plate** on a market stall — a single crumb, a smear of filling. Nothing else in frame.
- **ACT:** Hold 1 s. Then a fast pull-back: CHIEF's face fills frame right, mouth open in a theatrical gasp, one white glove raised.
- **CAM:** ECU static → fast PULLOUT to MED.
- **VO:** *"At eleven o'clock this morning, a crime was committed."*
- **SFX:** Silence → a single dramatic sting on the gasp. BGM enters immediately after.
- **EMO:** CHIEF scandalised.
- **RET:** **The hook.** Opens mid-crisis with an unanswered question and zero setup.

#### S2 — SCENE + THE TWO SEEDS (0:03–0:07)
- **VIS:** Wide 16:9 market square: a pie stall centre, `POP_TEAL` awning, crates, bunting. PIP sits on a low crate at frame left with a biscuit. BUD dozes on a short lead by a post. MITTENS the cat sits on a crate frame right. A small desaturated market crowd in the background.
- **ACT:** CHIEF plants his boot on a crate and, without looking, **leans his heavy ledger against the stall's table leg** (**SEED A** — the table tips a few degrees) and **sets his open evidence box down on the ground behind the stall** (**SEED B**). Both actions are casual, incidental, and completely clear.
- **CAM:** Static wide. Hold long enough that both seed actions read.
- **VO:** *"The victim: one pie. The scene: the market square."*
- **SFX:** BGM (market bed). A wooden *clunk* as the ledger lands; a light *creak* as the table tips; a soft *thud* for the box.
- **EMO:** CHIEF self-important · PIP oblivious.
- **RET:** Everything the audience needs to solve it is now on screen. The whole video is a fair-play mystery from here.

#### S3 — THE QUESTION (0:07–0:12)
- **VIS:** CHIEF whips out an oversized magnifying glass and a tiny notebook.
- **ACT:** He strikes a detective pose — glass up, chin high — and sweeps the square with the lens, his eye comically huge through it. The crowd leans in.
- **CAM:** MED, slow push-in.
- **VO:** *"And in the absence of anyone qualified… he appointed himself."*
- **SFX:** BGM + a bright investigative "sleuth" motif enters; notebook flick; a comic lens *whoosh*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF smug.
- **RET:** **Open loop planted** — who took it? The lens sweep invites the viewer to start looking too.

### ACT 2 — SUSPECT 1: BUD (0:12–0:28)

#### S4 — ACCUSATION 1 (0:12–0:17)
- **VIS:** CHIEF's lens lands on BUD, asleep, tail twitching.
- **ACT:** Gasp. He spins and **points** at BUD with his whole arm, medals jangling. BUD sleeps on.
- **CAM:** Whip-pan to BUD, then MED two-shot.
- **VO:** *"Suspect one. Motive: obvious."*
- **SFX:** BGM + accusation sting; a dog-snore.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant · BUD asleep.
- **RET:** First concrete answer offered — the audience gets a candidate to test.

#### S5 — DISPROOF 1 (0:17–0:23)
- **VIS:** Magnifier close on BUD's muzzle: **spotless**. Pull to show BUD's **short lead**, taut, ending well before the table.
- **ACT:** CHIEF's grin sags. He tugs the lead, measures the gap with two fingers, and deflates.
- **CAM:** ECU on muzzle → MED reveal of the lead.
- **VO:** *"Muzzle: clean. Lead: three feet. Table: nine."*
- **SFX:** BGM + magnifier *ting*; a lead *twang*; a small deflating "wah".
- **EMO:** CHIEF disappointed · BUD unbothered.
- **RET:** Micro-question answered → immediately reopens the main loop.

#### S6 — MICRO-PAYOFF 1 (0:23–0:28)
- **VIS:** CHIEF crosses a line in his notebook with an enormous flourish.
- **ACT:** BUD wakes, yawns hugely, and goes back to sleep — pure deadpan indifference.
- **CAM:** MED, static. **At 0:26: behind CHIEF's shoulder, a thin `ALERT_RED`-ish filling smear on the table's back edge flickers into view for ~3 frames, partly occluded by his elbow.** *(seed glimpse)*
- **VO:** — (let the visual land)
- **SFX:** BGM + notebook scratch; a long comic yawn; a tiny "boop" button.
- **EMO:** CHIEF frustrated · BUD deadpan.
- **RET:** **Laugh delivered at 0:26.** The seed glimpse plants unease without giving the answer.

### ACT 3 — SUSPECT 2: MITTENS (0:28–0:44)

#### S7 — ACCUSATION 2 (0:28–0:33)
- **VIS:** CHIEF spins 180° and locks onto MITTENS, who is sitting perfectly still on a crate, staring back.
- **ACT:** A much bigger, more operatic point — he steps up onto a crate to do it.
- **CAM:** Low angle on CHIEF for maximum pomposity, then cut to a flat MED on the cat.
- **VO:** *"Suspect two. Silent. Watchful. Suspiciously clean."*
- **SFX:** BGM swells + a bigger accusation sting; a single flat "meow".
- **EMO:** CHIEF theatrical · MITTENS blank.
- **RET:** **Pattern interrupt #1** — new suspect, new location in frame, bigger performance, landing exactly at the ~0:30 drop-off point.

#### S8 — DISPROOF 2 (0:33–0:39)
- **VIS:** CHIEF holds a tape measure between MITTENS and the pie plate — the cat is comically smaller than the plate.
- **ACT:** He looks at the tape, at the cat, at the tape again. He mimes the cat carrying the pie and physically cannot make the shape work.
- **CAM:** MED-WIDE, static, with a 4-frame hold on the mime.
- **VO:** *"Suspect two is four inches tall. The pie was nine."*
- **SFX:** BGM + tape-measure *zip* and *snap*; a small "nope" honk.
- **EMO:** CHIEF exasperated · MITTENS indifferent.
- **RET:** Physical-comedy beat; the mime is a visual gag that needs no language.

#### S9 — MICRO-PAYOFF 2 (0:39–0:44)
- **VIS:** Second line crossed off. The notebook now has two crossings and no answers.
- **ACT:** MITTENS licks a paw, utterly unconcerned, and turns away. CHIEF's mustache twitches.
- **CAM:** MED static. **At 0:41: in the background the table's tilt is now visibly greater, and a single drop of filling falls off the back edge — occluded to ~50% by a crate.** *(seed glimpse)*
- **VO:** — (let it land)
- **SFX:** BGM + notebook scratch; a cat paw-lick; one small "drip" (mixed low, easy to miss).
- **EMO:** CHIEF frustrated · MITTENS aloof.
- **RET:** **Laugh delivered at 0:41.** Frustration escalation raises the stakes for Act 4.

### ACT 4 — THE CASE AGAINST PIP (0:44–1:08)

#### S10 — ACCUSATION 3 (0:44–0:50)
- **VIS:** CHIEF turns and finds PIP quietly eating his own biscuit — **crumbs on his teal scarf**.
- **ACT:** The slowest, largest gasp yet. He advances on PIP, lens first. PIP freezes mid-bite.
- **CAM:** Slow PUSHIN on the crumbs, then MED two-shot.
- **VO:** *"And then — a break in the case."*
- **SFX:** BGM shifts to a darker, driving investigative variant + a big discovery sting; a tiny crumb *crunch*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF electrified · PIP alarmed.
- **RET:** The stakes become **personal and unjust** — the strongest retention driver in the video, because now the viewer wants to see PIP cleared.

#### S11 — THE ABSURD CASE (0:50–0:56)
- **VIS:** CHIEF slams up a corkboard: photos of PIP, of BUD, of the cat, of the pie plate, connected by a mad web of red string, every thread converging on PIP. No text — just images, string and pins.
- **ACT:** He gestures across it like a professor, tapping three points in sequence.
- **CAM:** MED-WIDE, slight push-in; 3-frame hold on each tap.
- **VO:** *"He had crumbs. He had proximity. He had, and I quote, 'a look about him.'"*
- **SFX:** BGM (driving) + three pin *thunks*; string *twang*; a pompous brass stab.
- **EMO:** CHIEF grandiose · PIP shrinking.
- **RET:** Peak absurdity — the comedy of the escalation carries the beat while the injustice builds.

#### S12 — PEAK INJUSTICE (0:56–1:02)
- **VIS:** CHIEF produces **the giant stamp** (a direct v1 callback) loaded with an `ALERT_RED` mark, and raises it high over PIP.
- **ACT:** PIP shrinks into a tiny ball, eyes welling. BUD whines. The crowd holds its breath. The stamp hangs at the top of its arc.
- **CAM:** Low angle on the raised stamp, PIP tiny at the bottom of frame. **At 0:58: reflected in the stamp's polished surface, a smear of filling and the corner of the evidence box are visible for ~4 frames.** *(seed glimpse — the answer is literally reflected in his weapon)*
- **VO:** *"Case closed."*
- **SFX:** BGM builds to its highest tension point + a rising *whoosh* on the stamp lift; a tiny PIP whimper; crowd inhale.
- **EMO:** CHIEF triumphant · PIP teary.
- **RET:** **Pattern interrupt #2** at the ~1:00 drop-off point. Maximum jeopardy — nobody leaves now.

#### S13 — THE QUIET POINT (1:02–1:08)
- **VIS:** PIP, cornered, does not plead. He simply raises one small arm and **points past CHIEF**, off toward the back of the stall.
- **ACT:** CHIEF, mid-gloat, doesn't even look — he lifts the stamp *higher*. PIP keeps pointing. BUD's head turns to follow PIP's finger. Then MITTENS looks. Then two of the crowd look.
- **CAM:** MED two-shot; a slow rack of attention as one by one the background characters turn.
- **VO:** — (deliberately silent; the pointing is the line)
- **SFX:** BGM begins to thin out — instruments dropping away one by one as more characters turn to look.
- **EMO:** CHIEF oblivious · PIP calm and certain.
- **RET:** **The turn.** Everyone on screen now knows except CHIEF — the audience is desperate for him to look.

### ACT 5 — SILENCE & REVEAL (1:08–1:30)

#### S14 — THE SILENCE (1:08–1:18)
- **VIS:** **MUSIC CUTS OUT COMPLETELY.** CHIEF finally lowers the stamp and follows PIP's finger. The camera travels with his eyeline: the **tilted table** → the **trail of filling** running down its back edge → over the lip → down to the ground → and settles on **his own open evidence box**.
- **ACT:** Slow, dreadful, unbroken. His face drains across the whole 10 seconds.
- **CAM:** One continuous slow **TRACK** along the eyeline. No cuts. 10 full seconds.
- **VO:** (silence — protect it) optional whisper at ~1:16: *"…oh no."*
- **SFX:** **Silence.** Permitted only: a distant market murmur, one wooden *creak* from the tilted table (~1:12), and a single *drip* (~1:15).
- **EMO:** CHIEF dawning horror · PIP calm.
- **RET:** The 10-second silence is the highest-tension stretch in the video and the payoff for everything.

#### S15 — THE REVEAL (1:18–1:25)
- **VIS:** Inside CHIEF's open evidence box: **the pie**, intact, sitting neatly, one slice-shaped dent where it landed. Pull back to reveal the full geometry — **his heavy ledger propped against the table leg**, the table tipped, the filling trail leading from plate to box like an arrow.
- **ACT:** CHIEF's face runs the **3-stage snap** triumphant → shocked → panicked. The giant stamp slips from his glove and lands with a thud, marking his own boot. The corkboard's red strings sag.
- **CAM:** ECU on the pie in the box → **PULLOUT** to a full wide showing ledger, table, trail and box in one frame; ~0.7 s freeze on that full-geometry wide.
- **VO:** *"There was no thief. There was a man who put his ledger down."*
- **SFX:** **BGM SLAMS BACK** (full stinger) + a comic reveal *ta-da* undercut by a record-scratch; the stamp *thud*; string *sag*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF mortified · PIP vindicated.
- **RET:** **The recontextualisation.** 78 seconds of investigation are re-read in one shot — the replay trigger fires here.

#### S16 — PAYOFF / LOOP (1:25–1:30)
- **VIS:** Composition returns to the **S1 framing** — the same stall, the same plate. PIP calmly sets the recovered pie back on the plate and hands CHIEF the empty box. BUD receives one slice. MITTENS gets a crumb. The crowd applauds PIP silently.
- **ACT:** CHIEF stands holding his own evidence box, mortified, the red stamp mark still on his boot. PIP turns and gives a small wave to camera. BUD's tail wags once.
- **CAM:** Wide matching S1's plate, then a slow settle into the **exact S1 ECU on the plate** as the final frame.
- **VO:** *"Every time."*
- **SFX:** BGM warm resolve; a plate *clink*; a happy dog *munch*; button *ding*.
- **EMO:** CHIEF sheepish · PIP relieved.
- **RET:** **Loop trigger** — the final frame is the first frame. Viewers who rewatch now catch the ledger at 0:05, which is exactly the behaviour this episode is built to produce.

## Compliance checklist (do not ship if any fails)
