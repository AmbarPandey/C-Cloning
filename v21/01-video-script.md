## Scene-by-scene

### C1 — COLD PAYOFF (0:00–0:01.5)
- **VIS:** ECU on a balloon lion's mane, **already mid-swell** — the sculpted lobes stretching smooth, one ear rounding away. Rubber under visible tension. No stand, no faces.
- **CAM:** Tight ECU, static, 45 frames. Motion underway on frame 1.
- **VO:** *"How big would you like it?"*
- **SFX:** No music. `SFX_rubber_creak_v1`, rising.

### C1b — GOAL DIAGRAM (0:01.5–0:02)
- **VIS:** Flat schematic: a round pressure gauge with a needle at rest and a bold `ALERT_RED` **max line** across its upper arc; beside it a small pictogram — one puff symbol, an `INK` equals sign, one twist/knot symbol.
- **CAM:** Flat MED, dead centre, held 15 frames.
- **SFX:** Music **enters** on the cut. `SFX_ting_v1`.

### C2 — REWIND / SETUP (0:02–0:06)
- **VIS:** A balloon-sculpture stand. `POP_TEAL` striped awning, a hand pump with the gauge mounted on it, a rack of uninflated balloons.
- **ACT:** PIP receives a crisp, small, perfectly-formed lion and holds it happily. CHIEF sneers at its size, seizes the pump, and — **jamming the `ALERT_RED` release valve lever shut with one glove** (**SEED B**, casual, unremarked) — starts pumping.
- **CAM:** MED two-shot, slow push-in ending on the jammed valve lever.
- **VO:** *"One of them said small."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_pump_v1` ×2 · `SFX_valve_click_v1` · `SFX_button_v1` "pfft"
- **EMO:** CHIEF `gloating` / PIP `neutral`

### C3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:06–0:11)
- **VIS:** His sculpture grows — a fine, grand lion with a full mane. The gauge needle climbs past halfway.
- **ACT:** He gestures at his own, then dismissively at PIP's small one. His eyeline stays on PIP, never on the gauge.
- **CAM:** Static; 4-frame hold as the needle passes the midpoint.
- **VO:** *"The other one said bigger."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_pump_v1` (rhythmic) · `SFX_needle_tick_v1` ×3

### C4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:11–0:16)
- **VIS:** Bigger again. The needle **crosses the `ALERT_RED` max line** and keeps going. The jammed valve does not vent. The lion is now huge and still recognisably magnificent.
- **ACT:** Pumping two-handed, chest out. Still not looking at the gauge.
- **CAM:** Slight push-in; 3-frame hold as the needle crosses the line.
- **VO:** *"And bigger."*
- **SFX:** `SFX_pump_v1` (fast) · `SFX_needle_tick_v1` past the line · `SFX_rubber_creak_v1` (enters low) · `SFX_button_v1` fanfare stab

- **VIS:** He hoists the enormous lion overhead in a triumphant pose, `FX_sparkle_v1` accents. **It is genuinely, impressively good** — a proper sculpture. He has won the transaction outright.
- **ACT:** Long held tableau, ~1.5 s freeze. Sell it completely; the reversal needs a real sculpture to destroy.
- **CAM:** Low hero angle, push-in settling into the hold.
- **VO:** *"Magnificent."* → stop.
- **SFX:** Bed peaks, **CUTS TO SILENCE mid-phrase**. Only `SFX_pump_v1`, still running, and `SFX_rubber_creak_v1`.

- **ACT:** PIP looks up. The pump is still going because the valve is jammed.
- **CAM:** Static, then slow tilt up to the deforming sculpture.
- **VO:** (silence) optional whisper *"…the valve."*
