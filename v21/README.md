**Story:** a balloon-sculpture stand with a pressure gauge and a max line. PIP asks for a small one and
gets a crisp little lion. CHIEF demands more air, then more, and jams the release valve shut so nothing
can stop — and past the max line his magnificent lion **doesn't burst.** It slowly, silently swells into a

## What's new
- **First `TW7` Visual Transformation** — the only twist container in the library whose payoff is a *shape change* rather than an event, which makes it the most mute-readable twist available and a natural fit for a format watched with sound off.
- **The most literal progress bar in the series:** a needle climbing toward a drawn red line. The retention analysis asked for the win condition to be *drawn*; a gauge is that request answered exactly.
- **`SC2` reused with a different pattern and a different arena** from v6's sweet stall — `NP1×SC2` is a fresh pair, and the mechanism (pressure) shares nothing with v6's (arithmetic).

## The decision I want to flag: no pop
The obvious ending is a burst. I didn't write one, and the constraint is enforced in all three files —
**there is no burst state, no pop asset, no debris.** Three reasons:
1. **It's funnier.** A pop is one loud instant. A slow smoothing is six seconds of dread the audience can see arriving and the character cannot — which is the dramatic-irony engine the analysis identified as v3's strongest asset.
2. **It's the twist core.** `TW7` is *transformation*, not destruction. A burst would be a different twist wearing this one's name — exactly the "core collapse" defect the base audit flagged on v7 and four of the v8–v17 batch.
3. **It's the ad-safety choice.** No bang, no startle, nothing that reads as breakage.

## The audio consequence
Every prior episode peaks on an impact — splash, crash, snap, gloop, hollow rattle. **v21 peaks on a
duration:** one long low `SFX_rubber_stretch_v1` groan with no transient in it. And the signature inverts
into the valve *sighing open* at 0:31 — the release he jammed shut, arriving one beat too late.
