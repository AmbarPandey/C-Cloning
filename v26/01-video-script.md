### C1 — COLD PAYOFF (0:00–0:01.5)
- **VIS:** ECU on a boat's `ASPHALT` gunwale sitting **flush with the flat `SKY` water surface**, with a desaturated duck silhouette **already mid-step** over it, one webbed foot on the wood. No pond, no faces, no context.

- **ACT:** PIP sets two light `PAPER` bundles onto his raft and steps aboard; it rides high. CHIEF sneers and **starts piling cargo crates into the big boat** (**SEED B**, casual, unremarked) — the hull already sitting lower.
