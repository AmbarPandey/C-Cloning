# Stage 1.5 — Business Decisions

## Purpose
Transform competitor intelligence into **one optimized execution strategy**. This stage makes the founder-level decisions — decisively, with weighted scoring — about exactly what to build.

## Inputs
- [Stage 1](10-stage-1-competitor-intelligence.md) competitor intelligence.

## Outputs
- A weighted business decision matrix ranking all competitor models.
- The chosen channel concept ("Channel DNA").
- A content factory design, viral formula, retention blueprint, asset library, AI tool stack, risk analysis, and a 90-day roadmap.

## The decision matrix

Six competitor models were scored across 18 weighted business criteria (viral potential, production speed, AI compatibility, scalability, monetization, advertiser-friendliness, evergreen value, cost, global reach, etc.).

| Rank | Model | Weighted score | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥇 1 | Frame Order / Cartoon Box (twist-ending) | 7.98 | **WINNER** |
| 2 | #5 high-volume factory ⚠️ | 7.68 | Validates the volume system |
| 3 | Rico (meme reaction) | 6.91 | Best raw speed, weak RPM |
| 4 | Osjtroubleson (relatable skits) | 6.86 | Best loyalty + funnel |
| 5 | Suppandi (IP episodic) | 5.67 | Best safety, slow to produce |
| 5 | Kheops (curated) ⚠️ | 5.67 | Not a buildable model |

## The core decision
Build on the **twist-ending engine**, fused with the best traits of the losers:
- From Suppandi/OSJ → a **recurring lovable cast**.
- From Rico/#5 → **crude-fast AI art + high cadence + repeatable template**.
- Our own edge → **advertiser-safe dark-lite humor** for higher RPM.

> The moat is **writing the twist**, not animation polish — which is exactly what AI makes cheap.

## Channel DNA (locked)

| Attribute | Decision |
|---|---|
| Concept | Twist-ending comedy shorts with a small recurring cast in everyday situations ending in an absurd but bloodless surprise |
| Brand / channel name | **IPPA** (locked — see [Channel Identity Lock](../channel/01-CHANNEL-IDENTITY-LOCK.md)) |
| Animation | Clean flat-color 2D, thick outlines, minimal shading |
| Humor | Dark-**lite** absurdist twist comedy, advertiser-safe |
| Voice | ElevenLabs primary; minimal, visual-led dialogue |
| Cast | 3–4 fixed, expression-first characters |
| Length | Core 45–90s; Phase-1 Shorts <40s |
| Cadence | Daily Short + 3 longs/week (Phase 1 = Shorts only) |
| Audience | Global, 16–34, meme-native |
| Palette | Yellow `#FFD400`, coral `#FF4D4D`, off-white `#FAF7F0`, ink `#1A1A1A`, teal `#00C2A8` |

## AI tool stack (grounded in mid-2026 research)

| Job | Chosen tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Animation / character consistency / lip-sync | **Anijam AI** | End-to-end script→screen with a timeline editor |
| Voice | **ElevenLabs** | Best quality + API scale; clone fixed cast voices |
| Image / backgrounds / props | Top-tier image generator | Style-locked flat art |
| Scripts / ideas | GPT-class LLM with a custom twist prompt | Human owns the final punchline |
| Music / SFX | AI music + owned cleared library | Zero copyright risk |
| Automation | n8n / Make.com | Chains idea → upload |

> Estimated stack cost: ~$120–250/month at Phase 1. Content on tools was rephrased for licensing compliance.

## Risk analysis (top risk)

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monetization rejection of AI/"reused" content** | High | Critical | Original scripts, original recurring cast, human-edited punchlines, original VO — engineered in from video 1 |

Other tracked risks: algorithm de-prioritization, copyright, AI-detection, saturation, trend dependency, production bottlenecks, viewer fatigue, scaling quality.
